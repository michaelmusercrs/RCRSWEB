01 The retail closing presentation

The close doesn't reveal the price — it earns it.

  1. Recap what you found — re-live the problem with photos
  2. Show them their roof — drone shots, the report
  3. Present the system, not just a shingle
  4. Anchor to the warranty — IKO ROOFPRO Craftsman Premier
  5. Then the investment — total paired with financing
  6. Ask for the business — then go quiet
01 Set the room before you present
Non-negotiable
ALL decision-makers present. Every time. No exceptions.

If both spouses (or all owners) aren't there — you reschedule. A "let me talk to my wife" isn't an objection, it's a sit-down you never should have taken. Confirm it when you book the appointment and again the day before.

+Present in person — the folder, photos, samples, and the handshake don't travel over email.
01 Ask, then handle the three you'll hear
  • "It's a lot of money." → cost-of-waiting + the monthly payment
  • "We want other quotes." → compare the whole system & who holds the warranty; hand them the folder
  • "We need to think about it." → "Is it the timing, the investment, or the roof itself?" then let silence work
Assumptive close
With our Craftsman Premier warranty you're covered long after the job's done, and we can start next week. Do you want the standard architectural, or the upgraded system I showed you?
02 Estimating gutters & LeafX

A new roof is the best time to sell gutters & protection.

  • New shingles into 20-year-old gutters is a callback waiting to happen
  • LeafX = the gutter-guard upsell — sell the outcome: never clean gutters again
  • Bundle it — one trip, one crew, one invoice; better than calling a gutter company later
Add-on tie-in
While the crew's already here, this is the one time it's cheap to do the gutters and put LeafX on them — and it protects the roof you just invested in.
02 Measure it right — on-site
  • Total linear feet of gutter (walk the eaves or pull the aerial)
  • Gutter size — 5" vs 6" changes gutter and guard price
  • Downspouts — count them, note 2-story runs
  • LeafX linear feet — priced per LF; can be a subset (tree side only)
  • Fascia / soffit rot — flag it now, not as a change order
$Pull per-LF numbers from the current price sheet — never quote gutter or LeafX from memory. A wrong measurement comes straight out of your margin.
03 The homeowner folder

Don't hand it over — walk them through it, page by page.

  • Signed agreement & scope — what they bought, in writing
  • Warranty & certification — IKO ROOFPRO + workmanship
  • The roofing checklist — every quality step on their roof
  • What-to-expect / prep sheet — noise, timeline, cars out
  • Care & maintenance + contacts
  • Review & referral card — plant the seed now
03 The checklist is the spine of the job

Read it out loud with the homeowner so they hear every quality step you're committing to. Same document, used twice — expectation-setter at signing, inspection script at the walkaround.

Pre-frame the walkaround
At the end of install day, we'll walk this exact list together — before you pay a dime.
04 Roof install day — before the crew starts

The homeowner bought you. Be there at the start.

  • Arrive at material drop / crew arrival — introduce crew to homeowner, confirm scope
  • Homeowner prep: cars out, furniture moved, pets in, kids clear
  • Protect: tarps on landscaping & AC, pool covered, wall pictures down
  • Set expectations: "It'll be loud, it's normal, done in a day, I'll be back to walk it."
04 The order of operations
  1. Stage & protect — tarps, materials up
  2. Tear-off — down to the deck
  3. Deck inspection — call out bad decking
  4. Dry-in — ice-and-water + underlayment (weather-tight)
  5. Install — drip edge, field, flashing, boots, vents, ridge
  6. Cleanup — haul, clear gutters, magnet sweep
!Decking = the honesty moment. Communicate bad decking and the price before it's replaced. Surprises on the invoice kill referrals.
05 The end-of-day walkaround
The whole lesson
Do it while the roofers are STILL on the job.

Crew still on site = same-day fix. Crew gone = a second trip, a frustrated customer, and delayed payment. Schedule your day to be on-site as the job wraps.

05 Walk it WITH them — checklist in hand
  • Magnet sweep — whole yard, drive, beds, both fence sides
  • Gutters cleared of debris & granules
  • Ground & roof clear; trailer ready to haul
  • Flashing, pipe boots, valleys sealed
  • Ridge vent / cap straight & complete
  • Drip edge & lines straight from the ground
  • Landscaping, AC, structures undamaged
  • Homeowner has seen it & is happy
★Point out quality as you walk, not just problems. Anything they spot — get the crew on it now.
06 Collecting final payment

Payment follows acceptance — collect it while you're standing there.

  • Balance due at completion — after they walk the roof & confirm they're happy
  • Know their method before you arrive — check, card, or financing draw
  • Tie it to the completion form — sign-off and settle in the same sit-down
  • Don't leave the job open — a finished roof with an uncollected balance is unfinished
Ask without flinching
Everything's done and you're happy — let's take care of the balance and I'll finalize your completion paperwork and warranty. Check today, or on the card?
07 Customer acceptance & reviews

Peak happiness is right now. Ask.

  • Get the acceptance / completion form signed — protects customer, company, warranty
  • Ask for the Google review out loud — it's where we're most beatable; pull it up on their phone for them
The review ask
One thing that genuinely helps a local company — would you take 60 seconds for a Google review while it's fresh? Here, I'll pull it up right now.
07 Getting referrals
  • Be specific — "Which neighbor has an older roof or lost shingles in that storm?" beats "know anyone?"
  • Hand over the referral cards — give them two or three
  • Ask for the yard sign — a billboard on a fresh, clean roof
  • Reference the current referral program — confirm it live with the office, never promise a number you're unsure of
Remember
The sale doesn't end at payment. It ends at the referral.
