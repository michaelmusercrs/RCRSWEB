Raise the Roof — Community Program

Giving back to local school sports teams · program plan & landing-page concept · draft 2026-07-27
What this is: a framework to formalize and amplify River City's existing "Raise the Roof" support of local school sports teams, and to wire it into the Smith Lake campaign as a genuine trust-and-goodwill driver. CONFIRM specifics — the exact schools, donation amounts, and mechanics below are placeholders for Michael to set; nothing here should be published until confirmed (no invented figures).

1 · The Concept

"Raise the Roof" is a double meaning — raising a new roof, and raising up the local teams. For qualifying roofs sold in a community, River City contributes to that community's school athletic programs, and celebrates it publicly. It converts a roofing transaction into local pride.

Mechanic option A — Per-job give-back

"For every roof we install in the [School District] area, River City donates $[amount] to [School] [Team]." Simple, trackable, ties donation to sales.

Mechanic option B — Season sponsorship

Flat seasonal sponsorship of [teams] (banners, jerseys, scoreboard, booster club) — steady presence regardless of sales volume.

Mechanic option C — Hybrid

Base sponsorship + a per-job "boost" that grows the pot as the community buys roofs — makes neighbors want you to win jobs.

2 · Why it sells roofs (not just goodwill)

  • Differentiator vs. storm-chasers. Out-of-town chasers descend after every storm. "We sponsor the [Mascot]" is something no fly-by-night can say. It pairs perfectly with Donnie's "lives on this lake, here since day one" story.
  • Referral flywheel. Parents, boosters, and coaches become advocates. A booster club with a River City banner is a warm-intro machine.
  • Yard-sign upgrade. Add a rider: "Proud supporter of [School] [Mascot]." On a rural county road, that turns a sales sign into a community sign people actually like seeing.
  • PR & social proof. Check presentations at games, Friday-night shout-outs, booster newsletters — earned local media with zero ad cost.
  • Warms the cold mail. The Smith Lake postcard/letter to a local owner hits different when it mentions their kid's team.
