5 · 90-Day Cadence

Phase 0 (Week 1) — load the guns: skip-trace all 561 (~$140); print letters + hail reports for remote owners; mail C-Wave 1 to all 384; Donnie starts knocking Crane Hill (fresh-local first).

Phase 1 (Weeks 1–4) — Crane Hill blitz: 3-pass canvass of the 173; EDDM week 2; yard signs up; week 3 phone follow-up on mailed owners ("did you get the storm report I mailed about the lake house?"). Target: 60+ inspections, 15+ claims filed.

Phase 2 (Weeks 5–8) — Double Springs clusters + C-Wave 2: canvass clusters #2/#3; postcard to all 384 + second letter to non-responders; premium letters to the 225; Crane Hill builds running → neighbor-knock + referral asks.

Phase 3 (Weeks 9–13) — sweep + second bite: canvass remaining clusters; re-knock "no answer ×3" with final hanger; C-Wave 3; phone sweep of non-contacts; re-approach every "roof's fine" if a new 2026 storm hits (log every storm date — it resets the conversation).

Three touches per target minimum. Locals: knock–hanger–knock. Remotes: letter–postcard–call.

6 · Insurance-Claim Workflow (inspection → job)

  1. Inspect & document: 20+ photos, chalked hits, test squares per slope, collateral (soft metals, gutters, screens, dock roof = waterfront bonus evidence). Pair with the per-property hail report.
  2. Kitchen-table verdict (in person, both decision-makers — the 80% rule): show photos on tablet. Honest fork — "don't file, this won't qualify" (builds referrals) or "this is legitimate, here's what filing looks like."
  3. Sign the contingency agreement BEFORE the claim call. Never waive/eat deductibles.
  4. Homeowner calls carrier with Donnie present — from the table; scheduled 3-way call for remote owners.
  5. Donnie meets the adjuster on-site — the core service promise. Walks the roof, presents documentation.
  6. Scope reconciliation: compare adjuster scope to ours; supplement legitimately (code items — drip edge, ice & water).
  7. Approved → build in 2 weeks → yard sign → neighbor knock → referral ask → review request. Denied → document, re-inspect after next storm, stay honest, stay in touch.
