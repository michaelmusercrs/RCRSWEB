WHAT TO CHECK TODAY (DIY)
================================================================================

1. OIL LEVEL
   - Pop hood, pull dipstick, check level
   - Should be between MIN and MAX marks
   - If low, add 0.5-1 quart and retest
   Time: 5 min, Cost: $0-20 (if you need oil)

2. SPARK PLUG #8
   - Location: Right side of engine, rear cylinder (cyl 8 = last one on right bank)
   - Tools: 5/8" spark plug socket, ratchet, gap tool
   - Remove plug, inspect for wear/carbon/fouling
   - Gap should be ~0.035" (check owner's manual)
   - If worn/dark/fouled, replace with OEM or quality equivalent
   Time: 30 min, Cost: $15-40 for plugs

3. MAF SENSOR
   - Location: On intake tube after air filter box
   - Tools: Socket set, MAF cleaner (NOT carb cleaner)
   - Unplug connector, unscrew sensor
   - Spray with MAF cleaner, let dry, reinstall
   Time: 15 min, Cost: $8-15 for cleaner

4. COIL PACK #8 (if spark plug replacement doesn't fix misfire)
   - Location: Top of engine, direct on spark plug well #8
   - Tools: Socket set, connector puller
   - Unscrew coil from cylinder #8
   - Check connector for corrosion/loose pin
   - If corroded, clean with electronics contact cleaner
   - If loose/damaged, replace coil pack
   Time: 30 min, Cost: $100-150 if replacement needed

================================================================================
FUEL TRIM EXPLAINED (FOR REFERENCE)
================================================================================

Short-Term Fuel Trim (STFT): Real-time adjustment by ECU
  Range: -25% to +25% is acceptable
  Your truck: -2.3% (lean at idle, ECU removing fuel)
  
Long-Term Fuel Trim (LTFT): Learned baseline adjustment
  Range: -10% to +10% is normal, ideally 0%
  Your truck: +9% (ECU learned engine wants extra fuel)
  
WHAT IT MEANS:
If LTFT is at +9%, ECU thinks you need 9% more fuel to run right.
This is caused by either:
1. Engine actually running lean (MAF, vacuum leak, bad sensor)
2. ECU over-correcting for misfire (thinks misfires are from leanness)

Once misfires are fixed, LTFT will reset to ~0% naturally after 100 miles of 
driving.
