📋 Program Rules & Definitions

Monthly Bonus Tiers — Trip Fund Pot Growth

Highest single tier reached in a month adds that bonus to your pot. Bigger month = bigger pot.

Hit $50K in a single month+$100 to pot
Hit $75K in a single month+$250 to pot
Hit $100K in a single month+$400 to pot
Hit $125K in a single month+$550 to pot
Hit $150K in a single month+$750 to pot
Hit $200K in a single month+$1,200 to pot
Hit $300K in a single month+$2,000 to pot
Hit $400K in a single month+$3,000 to pot
Hit $500K in a single month+$4,000 to pot
$500K → $1M (linear, +$1,000 per additional $100K)capped at +$9,000 (max @ $1M month)
Examples: $200K month = +$1,200 to pot. $700K month = $4,000 ($500K tier) + $2,000 (linear) = +$6,000 to pot. $1M month = +$9,000 (cap). Anything above $1M in a single month still tops out at $9,000.

Trip Rules & Restrictions

  • → Hit $400K cumulative Jan 1 – Jun 30 to qualify. $400K total must be reached — anything below = no payout, accrued bonuses forfeited.
  • → Trip must be scheduled 30–60 days following the end of the comp, unless approved by owners.
  • → RCRS books wherever rep wants. Total flexibility within budget — Frontier or first-class Delta is rep's call.
  • → Cash for small spend (excursions, on-the-ground items) issued before/during.
  • → H2 (Jul 1 – Dec 31) starts over for the next trip cycle.
  • → Sale Definition: a sale counts the month the contract is signed AND the deposit is received. The same revenue cannot be moved between months.

Cancellation / Returned Deposit

If a deposit is returned (deal canceled), revenue is removed from the month the deposit was originally received. Tier bonuses recalculate. If the cancellation drops the rep below qualification, the difference is clawed back from the trip pot. Sheet-side log captures every change.

Cash-Out Option (discretionary)

RCRS prefers the rep takes the trip. Break + cultural energy is worth more than cash. Cash-out is available case-by-case for qualified reps who can't travel.

Cash payout75% of trip pot
Why discounted?Encourages taking the trip

Example: $5,400 pot → trip 100% covered up to $5,400, OR $4,050 cash. Decided 1-on-1 with leadership. Not advertised as automatic.

Trip Spending — what's in

Total flexibility within accrued pot:

  • ✓ Flights (any class)
  • ✓ Hotel / accommodations (any tier)
  • ✓ Meals, drinks (any spend — $4K bottle of Bub if you want)
  • ✓ Excursions, activities
  • ✓ Ground transport (rental, Uber, etc.)
  • ✓ Cash for on-the-ground items

Tax note: trip value may be reportable to rep as taxable benefit. Discuss with bookkeeper before booking.
