# RCRS Sales Performance Guide

## Introduction

This guide covers the key metrics, benchmarks, and processes that drive sales performance at River City Roofing Solutions. Understanding these numbers is critical for every member of the team -- whether you are a new sales inspector or a seasoned manager.

## Key Performance Metrics

### Inspection Count
The number of inspections completed per week is the most fundamental leading indicator of sales success. More inspections mean more opportunities.

- **Target**: 8-10 inspections per week for full-time reps
- **Top performers**: 12-15 inspections per week
- **Tracked**: Weekly in the sales meeting

### Conversion Rate (Close Rate)
The percentage of inspections that result in a signed contract. This measures how effectively you turn opportunities into revenue.

- **Formula**: (Signed Contracts / Total Inspections) x 100
- **Strong**: 40-50%
- **Needs improvement**: Below 30%
- **Action**: If below 30% for 2+ weeks, review process with a manager

### Average Deal Size
Total revenue divided by number of closed jobs. Helps identify whether you are winning the right jobs.

- **Varies by territory and storm severity**
- **Track monthly** to spot trends
- **Higher is better**, but not at the expense of volume

### Speed to Lead
Time between receiving a new lead and making first contact. Faster response = higher close rates.

- **Target**: Under 30 minutes during business hours
- **Industry data**: Contacting a lead within 5 minutes is 10x more effective than waiting 30 minutes
- **After hours**: First thing next business morning

### Pipeline Value
The sum of potential revenue from all active leads and in-progress jobs.

- **Healthy pipeline**: 3-4x your monthly revenue target
- **Review weekly**: Are enough deals moving through each stage?
- **Stale deals**: Any lead sitting in one status for 2+ weeks needs action

### Revenue Per Inspection
Total revenue divided by total inspections. Measures efficiency.

- **Higher** = you are finding better opportunities and closing them at higher values
- **Lower** = you may be doing inspections that do not convert or underpricing jobs

### Referral Rate
Percentage of new business from past customer referrals.

- **Target**: 15-25% of new leads from referrals
- **Best indicator**: Customer satisfaction and long-term reputation
- **How to improve**: Ask every satisfied customer for referrals

## The Sales Pipeline

Every lead follows this pipeline. Keeping statuses updated in real time is mandatory.

1. **New Lead** - Just received, not yet contacted
2. **Contacted** - First call or text has been made
3. **Inspection Scheduled** - Date and time set
4. **Inspected** - Inspection completed, report provided
5. **Claim Filed** - Insurance claim submitted
6. **Approved** - Insurance approved the claim
7. **Work Scheduled** - Installation date is set
8. **Complete** - Job is finished

### Pipeline Health Indicators
- **Bottlenecks**: Too many leads stuck at one status = process issue
- **Velocity**: How fast leads move through stages (faster = better)
- **Drop-off points**: Where are leads falling out? That stage needs attention

## Weekly Sales Meeting Prep

Before each weekly meeting, know these numbers:
- How many inspections you completed this week
- Your conversion rate for the week
- Number of new contracts signed
- Pipeline value and stage breakdown
- Any deals that need help or escalation

## Setting Personal Goals

Every rep should set three weekly goals:
1. **Inspections**: How many will you complete?
2. **Claims filed**: How many claims will you submit?
3. **Contracts signed**: How many will you close?

Write them down Monday morning. Review them Friday afternoon. Adjust based on results.

## Improving Your Numbers

### If inspections are low:
- Generate more leads through door knocking and networking
- Respond faster to inbound leads
- Ask for referrals from completed jobs

### If conversion rate is low:
- Ride along with a top performer
- Review your inspection presentation
- Practice handling objections
- Ensure you are qualifying leads properly

### If deal size is low:
- Look for supplement opportunities
- Ensure complete and accurate scoping
- Do not leave money on the table -- check every line item

### If referral rate is low:
- Follow up with every completed customer
- Ask specifically: "Do you know anyone else who might need a roof inspection?"
- Make the referral process easy (referral cards, referral page link)

## Commission Tracking

Commissions are tied to completed and paid jobs:
- **Earned**: Job is complete and payment received
- **Pending**: Job is complete but payment not yet received
- **Projected**: Based on pipeline value and historical close rates

Track your year-to-date earnings monthly. Know your numbers.
