# RCRS Sales Territory & CRM Mastery Guide

## Territory Analysis & Planning

Effective territory management starts with understanding your area. Before knocking a single door, research your territory:

- **Storm history**: Check NWS hail reports and the RCRS "Check My Address" tool for recent storm events
- **Neighborhood age**: Homes 15-25 years old are prime targets -- shingles are nearing end of life
- **Roof types**: Focus on asphalt shingle neighborhoods; tile and metal are less common insurance claims
- **Saturation tracking**: Monitor what percentage of your territory you've canvassed to avoid overlap
- **Competition**: Note which competitors are active in your area and their approach

### Prioritizing Areas
1. Recently storm-impacted neighborhoods (within 6 months)
2. Older neighborhoods with aging roofs (15+ years)
3. Areas with known previous claims nearby
4. New subdivisions for maintenance inspections

## Storm Tracking & Hail Data

Understanding weather events is critical for proactive sales:

- **NWS Storm Reports**: National Weather Service publishes hail and wind reports daily
- **Hail size thresholds**: Quarter-sized (1 inch) or larger hail generally damages asphalt shingles
- **Wind damage**: Sustained winds over 60 mph can lift, crack, or remove shingles
- **Filing deadlines**: Most policies allow 1-2 years from the storm date to file
- **RCRS tools**: The /check-my-address page uses NWS data to show storm risk by address

### After a Storm Event
1. Review NWS reports for affected zip codes
2. Map your territory overlap with the storm path
3. Plan canvassing routes for the next 48 hours
4. Prepare storm-specific talking points for homeowners

## CRM Pipeline Management

JobNimbus is the backbone of your sales tracking. Keep it updated in real time.

### Pipeline Stages
- **New Lead**: Just received, not yet contacted. Respond within 30 minutes
- **Contacted**: First call/text made. Note the conversation details
- **Inspection Scheduled**: Date and time confirmed. Add to TeamUp calendar
- **Inspected**: Inspection complete, damage report filed. Upload photos
- **Claim Filed**: Insurance claim submitted. Track the claim number
- **Approved**: Insurance approved. Prepare the contract
- **Work Scheduled**: Installation date set. Coordinate with delivery
- **Complete**: Job finished. Begin follow-up sequence

### Pipeline Health
- **Stale leads**: Any lead sitting in one status for 2+ weeks needs action or removal
- **Bottlenecks**: Too many leads stuck at one stage = process issue
- **Velocity**: Track how fast leads move through stages (faster = better)

## Commission Structure & Tracking

Understanding your compensation is essential for goal setting:

- **Standard rate**: 8-12% of total job revenue
- **Volume bonuses**: Higher tiers available for monthly/quarterly volume milestones
- **Supplement commissions**: Additional commission earned on approved supplements
- **Referral bonuses**: Bonus for each qualified referral that results in a signed contract

### Commission Statuses
- **Projected**: Based on pipeline value and historical close rates
- **Pending**: Job complete, awaiting insurance payment
- **Earned**: Job complete and payment received
- **Paid**: Commission disbursed to the rep

### Tracking Your Numbers
- Review your commission report monthly
- Compare projected vs earned to identify gaps
- Track year-to-date earnings against personal goals

## Supplement Filing

Supplements are a significant revenue opportunity. They capture work that was missed or undervalued in the initial insurance estimate.

- **When to file**: After the initial estimate when you identify additional damage or missing line items
- **Documentation required**: Photos, measurements, Xactimate line items, and written justification
- **Common supplement items**: Drip edge, starter strip, ice and water shield, pipe boots, step flashing, code upgrades
- **Approval rate target**: Track your supplement approval rate -- aim for 80%+
- **Timing**: File supplements promptly; don't wait until the job is underway

## Canvassing & Door Knocking

Canvassing is the primary lead generation method for storm-affected areas:

### The Approach
1. **Introduction**: "Hi, I'm [name] with River City Roofing Solutions"
2. **Context**: "We're in the neighborhood because of the recent storm on [date]"
3. **Offer**: "We're offering free roof inspections to check for damage"
4. **No-pressure**: "Even if there's no damage, you'll have peace of mind"

### Best Practices
- Knock between 10 AM and 6 PM (respect quiet hours)
- Leave a door hanger if no one answers
- Note the address for follow-up
- Track your knock-to-inspection conversion rate
- Work systematically street by street

## Referral Generation

Referrals are the highest-converting lead source. Build a systematic approach:

- **Timing**: Ask after the customer confirms satisfaction (24-48 hours post-completion)
- **Script**: "Do you know anyone -- a neighbor, friend, or family member -- who might need a roof inspection?"
- **Make it easy**: Share the referral page link or give them referral cards
- **Follow up**: Thank the referrer when their referral converts
- **Target**: 15-25% of new business should come from referrals

## Inspection Best Practices

The inspection is your sales presentation. Prepare thoroughly:

- **Before arrival**: Review the property on Google Maps, check storm history for the address
- **On-site**: Introduce yourself, explain the process, get permission to access the roof
- **Documentation**: Photograph all damage with close-ups and wide shots
- **Presentation**: Show the homeowner the damage (from the ground or via photos)
- **Next steps**: Explain the insurance claim process and what happens next
- **Follow up**: Send a summary within 24 hours

## Weekly Goal Setting

Every Monday morning, set three concrete goals:

1. **Inspections**: How many will you complete this week? (Target: 8-10)
2. **Claims filed**: How many insurance claims will you submit?
3. **Contracts signed**: How many will you close?

Review progress daily. Adjust by Wednesday if you're falling behind. Review results Friday afternoon and carry forward to next week's plan.
