# RCRS Field Operations & Delivery Guide

## Material Ordering Process

Every material order flows through a structured process to ensure accuracy and timeliness:

### How to Place an Order
1. Navigate to `/portal/inventory`
2. Select "New Order" and link it to the job number
3. Choose materials from the catalog (shingles, underlayment, flashing, etc.)
4. Enter quantities based on the job scope (reference the estimate)
5. Set the requested delivery date (at least 24 hours out for standard orders)
6. Add special instructions (access issues, drop location preferences)
7. Submit -- this auto-creates a delivery ticket

### Order Priority Levels
- **Standard**: 24-48 hour delivery window
- **Rush**: Same-day or next-morning delivery (requires manager approval)
- **Emergency**: Active job is waiting on materials (highest priority)

## Delivery Lifecycle

Every delivery follows a defined lifecycle with status tracking:

### Status Definitions
- **Ordered**: The material order has been placed and confirmed with the supplier
- **Staged**: Materials have been pulled from warehouse inventory and are ready for loading
- **In Transit**: The delivery truck has departed the warehouse with the materials
- **Delivered**: Materials have arrived at the jobsite and been confirmed

### Status Updates
- Update statuses in real time through the delivery portal
- Each status change triggers notifications to the relevant team members
- Never skip a status -- go through each stage in order

## Loading Checklist

The loading checklist is mandatory before every delivery. It protects the company, the driver, and the customer:

### Checklist Items
1. **Material verification**: Every item on the delivery ticket is on the truck
2. **Quantity check**: Counts match the ticket exactly
3. **Quality inspection**: No damaged materials loaded
4. **Load security**: Materials are properly secured and won't shift during transit
5. **Photo documentation**: Photograph the loaded truck from multiple angles
6. **Address confirmation**: Delivery address is verified and entered in GPS
7. **Special instructions**: Review any notes about access, placement, or contact
8. **Safety equipment**: PPE is on the truck (hard hat, gloves, safety glasses, boots)

### If Something Doesn't Match
- Do NOT deliver incorrect materials -- it causes delays and double-handling
- Contact the office to resolve discrepancies before departing
- Document the issue in the delivery portal notes

## Inventory Management

Accurate inventory is critical to smooth operations:

### When to Update Inventory
- **Receiving**: When materials arrive from suppliers
- **Pulling**: When materials are pulled for a delivery
- **Delivering**: When materials leave the warehouse
- **Returning**: When unused materials come back from a jobsite

### Inventory Counts
- Spot-check high-volume items weekly
- Full inventory count monthly
- Report discrepancies immediately -- don't try to "fix" numbers

### Low Stock Alerts
- The system triggers alerts when stock drops below minimum levels
- Reorder points are set per material based on typical usage
- Don't wait for an alert -- report low stock if you notice it

## Route Planning & Optimization

Efficient delivery routes save time, fuel, and money:

### Route Planning Principles
- Group deliveries by geographic area
- Schedule the farthest delivery first if timing allows
- Account for traffic patterns (avoid rush hour for metro deliveries)
- Confirm customer availability before departing
- Allow time buffer between stops for unloading

### Using the Delivery Portal for Routes
- View all scheduled deliveries on the map view
- The system suggests route order based on distance
- Print or display the route on your mobile device
- Update ETA as you complete each stop

## Material Returns

Returns happen -- handle them properly:

### Return Process
1. Bring materials back to the warehouse
2. Document: what, how much, condition, and reason for return
3. Office staff inspects returned materials
4. Restockable items go back into inventory with updated counts
5. Damaged or non-returnable items are noted for disposal or supplier return

### Common Return Reasons
- Over-ordered materials
- Wrong color or type delivered
- Job scope changed after delivery
- Customer cancelled

## Safety Requirements

Safety is non-negotiable on every jobsite:

### Required PPE
- **Hard hat**: Always when materials are being loaded, unloaded, or moved overhead
- **Safety glasses**: Protects against debris during loading/unloading
- **Work gloves**: Required when handling materials
- **Steel-toe boots**: Mandatory on all jobsites and in the warehouse
- **High-vis vest**: Required when working near roads or in active construction zones

### Safe Lifting Practices
- Shingle bundles weigh 60-80 lbs each -- use proper lifting technique
- Lift with your legs, not your back
- Ask for help with heavy or awkward loads
- Use mechanical assistance (forklift, conveyor) when available

### Vehicle Safety
- Complete a pre-trip inspection daily
- Secure all loads before driving
- Follow all traffic laws -- you represent RCRS
- Report any vehicle issues immediately

## Delivery Documentation

Proper documentation protects everyone:

### Every Delivery Must Include
- Completed delivery ticket with all fields filled
- Photos of materials as loaded
- Photos of materials as delivered (at the jobsite)
- Customer signature if available (or note "left at site per instructions")
- Timestamp of delivery completion in the portal

### If Issues Arise
- Damaged materials: photograph, document, notify office
- Wrong address: contact office before unloading
- Access issues: document, photograph, contact office for guidance
- Customer complaint: remain professional, document, escalate to office

## Communication During Deliveries

Keep the team informed throughout the delivery process:

- **Departure**: Update status to "In Transit" when leaving the warehouse
- **ETA updates**: The system auto-notifies customers, but call if you'll be significantly early or late
- **Completion**: Update status to "Delivered" immediately upon completion
- **Issues**: Call the office immediately for any problems -- don't try to handle everything alone
