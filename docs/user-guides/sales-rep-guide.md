# Sales Representative Guide

## Overview

This guide covers the sales workflow and tools available to River City Roofing sales representatives. The system integrates with JobNimbus CRM for lead management and provides real-time sales tracking.

---

## Accessing Your Dashboard

### Command Center Access

Navigate to `/command-center/sales` to view:
- Your sales performance
- Team leaderboard rankings
- Active leads
- Commission tracking

### Login

1. Go to `/portal`
2. Select "Staff Login"
3. Enter your company email
4. You'll be redirected to your dashboard

---

## Sales Dashboard Features

### Sales Leaderboard (`/command-center/sales`)

The leaderboard displays:
- **Rankings**: Current standings among sales team
- **Monthly Sales**: Total sales this month
- **Year-to-Date**: Cumulative annual performance
- **Commission Balance**: Current earnings

### Individual Rep View (`/command-center/sales/[rep]`)

Your personal dashboard shows:
- Sales history
- Active prospects
- Scheduled appointments
- Commission details

### Achievements (`/command-center/sales/achievements`)

Track milestones:
- Sales targets met
- Performance badges
- Team recognitions

## Working with Leads

### Lead Sources

Leads come from multiple channels:
1. **Website Contact Form**: Submitted at `/contact`
2. **Referrals**: Customer referral program
3. **Door-to-Door**: Direct canvassing
4. **Storm Chasing**: Weather event follow-up
5. **Marketing Campaigns**: Ad responses

### Lead Notifications

When a new lead arrives:
1. GroupMe notification sent to team
2. Lead appears in JobNimbus
3. Lead syncs to Command Center

### Following Up

Best practices for lead follow-up:
1. Contact within 5 minutes of submission
2. Log all interactions in JobNimbus
3. Schedule inspection appointment
4. Update lead status promptly

---

## Scheduling Inspections

### TeamUp Calendar Integration

Schedule inspections through the system:

1. Navigate to `/command-center/schedule`
2. Select date and time
3. Enter customer information
4. Inspection event created in TeamUp

### Inspection Checklist

During inspections:
- [ ] Introduce yourself and company
- [ ] Assess roof condition
- [ ] Take photos (before state)
- [ ] Discuss findings with homeowner
- [ ] Provide estimate timeline
- [ ] Collect contact preferences

---

## Estimates & Proposals

### Creating Estimates

Estimates are managed in JobNimbus:
1. Create estimate in JobNimbus
2. Add line items for materials/labor
3. Generate PDF proposal
4. Send to customer

### Estimate Follow-up

Track estimate status:
- **Sent**: Awaiting customer review
- **Viewed**: Customer opened document
- **Approved**: Ready for contract
- **Declined**: Follow up for feedback

## Weather & Storm Response

### Weather Monitoring (`/command-center`)

The dashboard includes:
- Current weather conditions
- 7-day forecast
- Workability indicators
- Weather alerts

### Hail Reports

Access hail data for canvassing:
1. Check recent hail reports in your area
2. Filter by date range
3. View affected neighborhoods
4. Plan canvassing routes

## Commission Tracking

### How Commissions Work

Commissions are tracked via Google Sheets:
- Earned when job is sold
- Updated when job completes
- Balance available in dashboard

### Viewing Your Balance

1. Go to Command Center
2. View commission widget
3. See detailed transactions in reports

### Commission Reports

Access detailed commission history:
- Filter by date range
- View by job
- Export for records

## Best Practices

### Lead Response

- **5-Minute Rule**: Contact leads within 5 minutes
- **Log Everything**: Record all interactions
- **Set Reminders**: Schedule follow-ups

### Customer Communication

- Be professional and friendly
- Explain roofing terms simply
- Provide written estimates
- Follow up consistently

### Time Management

- Plan routes efficiently
- Batch appointments by area
- Use drive time for calls
- Update status in real-time

## Troubleshooting

### Not Seeing Leads

- Check JobNimbus sync status
- Verify your assignment
- Refresh dashboard

### Calendar Not Syncing

- Verify TeamUp integration
- Check for sync errors
- Contact admin if persistent

### Commission Discrepancy

- Review job status in JobNimbus
- Check commission calculation
- Contact office for review
