# Customer Portal Guide

## Welcome to Your Customer Portal

The River City Roofing Solutions Customer Portal gives you 24/7 access to your project information, appointments, and direct communication with our team.

---

## Accessing Your Portal

### Three Ways to Log In

Navigate to `/customer` and choose your login method:

**1. Email Login**
- Enter the email address associated with your project
- We'll match it to your account in our system

**2. Phone Login**
- Enter your phone number (format: 256-555-1234)
- We'll match it to your account

**3. Access Code**
- Enter the unique access code provided in your welcome email/text
- This is the fastest login method

### First-Time Access

When you first receive communication about your roofing project:
1. You'll receive an access code via email or text
2. Use this code at `/customer`
3. Your portal is immediately available

## Understanding Your Project Status

### Lead Phase
- We've received your information
- A team member will contact you
- **Your action**: Answer when we call

### Inspection Phase
- Roof inspection is scheduled or completed
- Inspector assesses your roof condition
- **Your action**: Ensure access to property

### Estimate Phase
- We're preparing your detailed quote
- Includes materials, labor, warranty
- **Your action**: Review when received

### Contract Phase
- Agreement has been signed
- Project is officially scheduled
- **Your action**: Confirm contact info is current

### Permit Phase
- Required permits being obtained
- Timeline varies by municipality
- **Your action**: None required

### Materials Phase
- Roofing materials are ordered
- Delivery to job site scheduled
- **Your action**: Ensure driveway access

### Scheduled Phase
- Installation date is confirmed
- Crew is assigned
- **Your action**: Prepare for installation day

### In Progress Phase
- Work is actively underway
- Daily progress being made
- **Your action**: Stay available for questions

### Complete Phase
- Project is finished
- Final inspection done
- **Your action**: Review work, provide feedback

## Appointments

### Viewing Appointments

Your portal shows:
- Date and time
- Type of appointment
- Assigned team member(s)
- Special instructions

### Appointment Types

**Inspection**
- Duration: ~1 hour
- What happens: Roof assessment
- Your presence: Helpful but not required

**Material Delivery**
- Duration: ~30 minutes
- What happens: Materials placed on property
- Your presence: Not required

**Installation**
- Duration: 1-3 days typically
- What happens: Full roof replacement/repair
- Your presence: Not required, but stay reachable

**Final Walkthrough**
- Duration: ~30 minutes
- What happens: Review completed work
- Your presence: Recommended

### Rescheduling

Need to change an appointment?
1. Message us through the portal
2. Or call 256-274-8530
3. We'll find a new time that works

## Preparing for Your Project

### Before Inspection

- Clear access to all roof areas
- Note any specific concerns
- Have insurance info ready (if claim)

### Before Material Delivery

- Clear driveway space
- Remove vehicles from delivery area
- Note any access restrictions

### Before Installation

- Move vehicles from driveway
- Remove outdoor furniture near work area
- Secure pets
- Inform neighbors about work

### During Installation

- Normal noise expected
- Workers may need water/restroom access
- Stay available by phone
- Debris cleanup included

## FAQs

### How long will my project take?
Most residential roof replacements take 1-3 days, depending on size and complexity.

### What if I'm not home during work?
That's fine! Just ensure property access. We'll update your portal with progress.

### Will you clean up debris?
Yes, cleanup is included. We use tarps and magnetic sweepers to collect all materials.

### What warranty do I get?
You'll receive both manufacturer material warranties and our workmanship warranty. Details in your contract.

### How do I report an issue after completion?
Message us through the portal or call 256-274-8530. We stand behind our work.
