# Delivery Driver Guide

## Overview

The Driver Portal is a mobile-optimized interface for delivery drivers to manage their daily deliveries, capture proof of delivery, and maintain communication with the office. Access the portal on your phone or tablet for best experience.

---

## Logging In

### PIN Login

1. Go to `/portal` on your mobile device
2. Tap "Driver Portal"
3. Enter your 4-digit PIN on the number pad
4. Tap "Sign In"

**Your PIN** was provided by the office. If you forget it, contact admin.

### First-Time Login

On your first login:
1. Complete the training popup
2. Review feature updates
3. You'll be redirected to your delivery list

## Today's Deliveries

### Delivery List View

- List of assigned deliveries

**Each delivery card displays:**

## Delivery Workflow

### Step-by-Step Process

```
1. Load Verified --> 2. Start Delivery --> 3. Mark Arrived -->
4. Complete Delivery --> 5. Capture Proof --> 6. QC Photos -->
7. Complete
```

### Step 1: Verify Load

Before leaving the yard:
1. Open the delivery in your app
2. Review the materials list
3. Physically verify all items are loaded
4. Tap "Verify Load"
5. GPS location is automatically captured

### Step 2: Start Delivery

When leaving the yard:
1. Tap "Start Delivery"
2. Status changes to "En Route"
3. Use the "Navigate" button for directions

### Step 3: Mark Arrived

When you reach the job site:
1. Tap "Mark Arrived"
2. GPS location is captured
3. Status updates to "Arrived"

### Step 4: Complete Delivery

After offloading materials:
1. Add any delivery notes (optional)
2. Tap "Complete Delivery"
3. Status changes to "Delivered"

### Step 5: Capture Proof

Get customer acknowledgment:
1. Tap "Capture Proof"
2. Enter the name of person receiving delivery
3. Capture signature (future feature)
4. Tap "Confirm Proof of Delivery"

### Step 6: QC Photos (If Required)

Take quality control photos:
1. Tap the camera icon
2. Photograph:
   - Materials placement
   - Job site condition
   - Any issues/concerns
3. Photos upload automatically

### Step 7: Complete

1. Tap "Complete" to finish the ticket
2. Return to your delivery list
3. Start next delivery

## Taking Photos

### Photo Types

- **Load Verification**: Before leaving
- **Delivery Proof**: Materials in place
- **QC Photos**: Quality documentation
- **Issue Photos**: Any problems

### How to Take Photos

1. Tap the camera icon
2. Allow camera access if prompted
3. Frame the shot clearly
4. Tap to capture
5. Photos upload automatically

**Photo Tips:**
- Ensure good lighting
- Include materials clearly
- Capture address numbers if visible
- Document any issues

## Handling Issues

### Customer Not Home

1. Call customer phone number
2. Leave materials if instructed
3. Add notes to delivery
4. Take photos of placement
5. Complete delivery with note

### Wrong Materials

1. Do NOT deliver wrong items
2. Call the office immediately
3. Document with photos
4. Return to yard if necessary
5. Add detailed notes

### Damaged Materials

1. Photograph damage immediately
2. Note extent of damage
3. Contact office for guidance
4. Document in delivery notes
5. May need replacement delivery

### Can't Access Property

1. Try calling customer
2. Check for gate codes in notes
3. Contact office
4. Document attempts
5. May need to reschedule

## Troubleshooting

### Can't Log In

- Verify your PIN is correct
- Check internet connection
- Try refreshing the page
- Contact office for PIN reset

### Deliveries Not Showing

- Pull down to refresh
- Check date filter
- Verify you're assigned deliveries
- Contact office if persistent

### Photos Not Uploading

- Check internet connection
- Wait for upload indicator
- Retry if needed
- Photos saved and retry automatically

### GPS Not Working

- Enable location in browser settings
- Refresh the page
- Try a different browser
- Location not required to continue

### App Running Slow

- Close other browser tabs
- Restart browser
- Clear browser cache
- Use Chrome for best performance
