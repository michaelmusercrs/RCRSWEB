# BNI Presentation - How to View & Edit

## Quick View (takes 30 seconds)

### Step 1: Open the Presentation
Go to this link in your browser (Chrome works best):

**https://www.rivercityroofingsolutions.com/bni/present-3**

That's it - the presentation is live on our website right now.

---

### Step 2: Navigate the Slides

| Action | How |
|--------|-----|
| Next slide | Press **right arrow key** OR **spacebar** OR click the right edge of the screen |
| Previous slide | Press **left arrow key** OR click the left edge of the screen |
| See speaker notes | Press **N** on your keyboard (press again to hide) |

There are **6 slides** total:
1. Title - "Technology That Protects Homeowners"
2. StormCheck - Free storm damage risk assessment
3. Storm & Hail Report - Insurance documentation
4. Customer Portal - Personal portal demo
5. IKO Roof Visualizer - See your roof before install
6. Close / Referral - $200 referral reward + contact info

---

### Step 3: Test the Demo Links

Each slide has a green button that opens a live demo. Try clicking them:
- Slide 2: "LIVE DEMO" opens our Check My Address tool

## How to Request Edits

The presentation is built into the website code, so to make changes, just tell Michael (or text/message him) what you want changed. Here's what CAN be changed easily:

### Things You Can Change
- Any text on any slide (titles, bullet points, descriptions)
- Speaker notes (the notes that appear when you press N)
- The $200 referral amount
- Phone number or email
- Which demos each button links to
- Add or remove bullet points
- Add or remove entire slides
- The date at the bottom of slide 1 (currently says "March 2026")

### How to Request a Change
Just say something like:
> "On slide 2, change 'Instant risk score with damage probability' to 'Instant risk score based on YOUR property's storm history'"

> "Add a new bullet to slide 6 that says 'Licensed and insured in Alabama and Tennessee'"

> "Change the speaker note on slide 1 to mention the BNI chapter name"

- Press **N** before you start to have speaker notes visible (only you can see them if your screen isn't projected)
