## The 9 Sections

1. **Public Website** — 300+ SEO-optimized pages driving leads 24/7 with contact forms, service pages, and local landing pages
2. **Check My Address** — Free hail damage reports for any address; captures homeowner leads automatically from NWS storm data
3. **Command Center** — Executive dashboard with real-time KPIs, revenue trends, rep performance, and team reports
4. **Sales Portal** — Mobile-first CRM for reps: lead pipeline, job status, commission tracker, quick-assign, and scheduling
5. **Office Portal** — Material orders, delivery scheduling, invoicing, and day-to-day operations management
6. **Delivery & Driver Portal** — Route sheets, loading checklists, ETA notifications, and proof-of-delivery capture
7. **Inventory Management** — Live stock tracking, low-stock alerts, transaction history, and purchase orders
8. **Billing & Invoicing** — Invoice creation, payment tracking, commission calculations, and financial reporting
9. **Training & Onboarding** — Sales courses, platform walkthroughs, quizzes (70% to pass), and printable certificates

- **Google Sheets** — Live data backend for forms, tracking, and reporting

- **GroupMe Chat** — Team and direct messaging built into the portal
- **Google Calendar** — Scheduling links for appointments, deliveries, and follow-ups
- **TeamUp** — Shared team calendar with bi-directional sync
- **NWS Weather Data** — Real-time storm and hail reports powering Check My Address
- **Google Analytics** — Full traffic and conversion tracking across all public pages
