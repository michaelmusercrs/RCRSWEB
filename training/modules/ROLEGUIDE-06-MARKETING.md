The central marketing operations center:

  - Instagram ads (stories, feed, reels concepts)

- Campaign performance tracking
- Budget monitoring per campaign/platform
- A/B test tracking

#### Email Marketing (/command-center/marketing/emails)
- 5 pre-built email templates:
  - Welcome/intro email
  - Storm season alert
  - Inspection follow-up
  - Referral program promotion
  - Seasonal maintenance tips
- Send history and tracking
- Open/click rate monitoring
- Template customization

#### Content Calendar (/command-center/marketing/calendar)
- Monthly content planning
- Post scheduling across platforms
- Blog publishing coordination
- Campaign launch dates
- Promotional event scheduling
- Seasonal content planning

#### Ad Management (/command-center/marketing/ads)
- Active campaign overview
- Performance metrics per ad
- Budget tracking and allocation
- Platform management

### Blog CMS (/portal/admin/blog)
- Create and edit blog articles
- 68 existing articles (SEO-optimized roofing, storm, insurance topics)
- Publish/unpublish controls
- Category management
- SEO settings per post (title, description, keywords)
- Image insertion and management

### Image Library (/portal/admin/images)
- Upload marketing assets (photos, graphics, logos)
- Organize by category
- Use in blog posts and marketing materials
- Vercel Blob storage

### Check My Address -- Lead Generation Tool
- /check-my-address -- public page that generates leads
- Homeowner enters address, gets a free hail risk report
- Auto-captures name, email, phone and creates a lead
- Can be promoted in:
  - Social media posts ("Check your address for FREE!")
  - Email campaigns
  - Door hangers and direct mail
  - Google/Facebook ads
  - BNI presentations
- Shows real NWS weather/hail data -- genuine value

### Public Website SEO
- JSON-LD structured data on every public page
- Schema types: LocalBusiness, Article, Service, FAQ
- Google Analytics (G-Y8PB85BZC5) -- track all website traffic
- 300+ indexed pages (massive SEO footprint)
- 68 blog articles driving organic traffic
- Location pages for Huntsville, Madison, Decatur, Birmingham, Nashville
- Service pages for every offering

### Referral Program (/referral-rewards)
- Public page promoting customer referrals
- Referral form captures referrer + referred info
- Rewards/incentives structure
- Submissions go to Google Sheets

### BNI Page (/bni)
- Business Networking International partnerships
- Referral program details for BNI members
- Chapter information

### Google Analytics Access
- Track page views across 367+ pages
- Monitor blog performance (which articles get traffic)
- Conversion tracking (form submissions)
- Traffic sources (organic, paid, referral, direct, social)
- Geographic data (where visitors are located)
- Device breakdown (mobile vs desktop)

### Team Profile Management
- Access to /portal/admin/team for updating team bios
- Public team pages drive trust and local SEO
- Each team member has a dedicated page with bio, photo, specialties

### Service Area Management
- Access to /portal/admin/areas
- 50+ cities and service areas configured
- Each area gets an SEO-optimized page
- Add new areas as company expands (Birmingham, Nashville)

## SECTION 4: HANDS-ON PRACTICE

### Exercise 1: Analytics Review (5 min)
1. Open Google Analytics (if access configured)
2. Check total page views for the past 7 days
3. Find the top 5 most-viewed pages
4. Check traffic sources breakdown
5. Note the mobile vs desktop split

### Exercise 2: Blog Post Creation (10 min)
1. Go to /portal/admin/blog
2. Click "Write New Post"
3. Title: "5 Signs Your Roof Needs Inspection After a Storm"
4. Write 3-4 paragraphs of content
5. Set category and SEO fields
6. Save as draft (don't publish yet)

### Exercise 3: Marketing Hub Tour (5 min)
1. Go to /command-center/marketing
2. Review the campaign overview
3. Click through to Ads page -- review ad variations
4. Click through to Emails page -- review templates
5. Click through to Calendar page -- see the content schedule

### Exercise 4: Check My Address Promotion (5 min)
1. Go to /check-my-address
2. Test it with your own address
3. Review the report output
4. Think about: How would you promote this on social media?
5. Draft a sample social media post promoting it

### Exercise 5: Image Library (5 min)
1. Go to /portal/admin/images
2. Browse existing images
3. Upload a test image
4. Practice organizing by category
5. Note which images are used in blog posts

### Exercise 6: Service Areas (5 min)
1. Go to /portal/admin/areas
2. Browse the 50+ configured cities
3. Click on a few to see their pages
4. Think about: Which new areas should we add for Birmingham/Nashville expansion?
