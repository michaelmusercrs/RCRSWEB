## SECTION 1: SYSTEM FEATURES & FUNCTIONS

### Your Sales Dashboard (Mobile-First!)
The sales portal is built mobile-first — use it on your phone in the field.

- **Welcome Header**: Your name + hot streak indicator when you're on a roll
- **Monthly Commission Progress**: Big visual progress bar showing how close you are to your monthly goal
- **Quick Stats Row**: Active Leads | Deals Closed | Your Rank | Commission $
- **Team Comparison Card**: Shows your percentage above/below team average
- **Commission Summary (3 columns)**:
  - Earned (green) — money in the bank
  - Pending (yellow) — deals closed, waiting on payment
  - Avg Deal Size — your typical deal value

### Quick Action Buttons (always visible)
- **Quick Call** — opens your priority contact list, tap to call
- **Schedule** — jump to calendar to book an inspection
- **Send Quote** — generate and send a quote
- **Upload Photo** — snap and upload inspection photos

### Priority Leads Section
Your hottest leads shown with urgency indicators (Urgent/High). Each lead has inline buttons:
- **Call** — one-tap dial
- **Text** — open text message
- **Send Portal** — share customer portal link
- **Details** — full lead information

### Recent Activity Feed
Timeline of your latest: commissions earned, deals closed, leads assigned, status changes

### Today's Inspections
Your scheduled appointments for the day with a Map button for navigation

### Bottom Mobile Navigation
5 tabs: Home | Leads | Quick Call (center, prominent) | Stats | Portal

---

### Lead Management
- **Your Lead List**: All leads assigned to you
- **Status Filters**: New → Contacted → Scheduled → Inspected → Quoted → Won / Lost
- **Search**: Find leads by name or address
- **Priority Sorting**: Urgent first, then high, then normal
- **Status Updates**: Tap to move a lead through the pipeline
- **Lead Preferences**: Set your territory, notification frequency, lead types

### Customer CRM (JobNimbus Connected)
When you click on a customer, you get a full 6-tab detail view:
1. **Overview** — contact info, address, job summary
2. **Active Jobs** — current projects in progress
3. **Job History** — past completed work
4. **Documents** — contracts, estimates, inspection photos
5. **Messages** — full communication log
6. **Transactions** — payments and invoices

Everything syncs with JobNimbus — update here, it updates there, and vice versa.

### Performance Dashboard
- Your personal KPIs: revenue, conversion rate, average deal size
- Monthly and yearly comparisons
- Goal tracking with progress bars
- Commission trends over time
- Your leaderboard position and movement (up/down arrows)

### Check My Address — Your Sales Superweapon
- Go to /check-my-address on your phone
- Enter a homeowner's address right at their door
- Show them their personalized hail/storm risk report
- Report shows: risk score, recent hail events, recommendations
- Use this to START conversations, not just close them
- Every report auto-creates a lead if they provide contact info

### Customer Portal Sharing
- When you win a deal, share a unique customer portal link
- Customer can track their job timeline, see delivery status, view documents
- Makes RCRS look professional and keeps customers informed
- No password needed — unique token URL

### GroupMe Team Chat
- Chat with the whole team or DM individuals
- @mention someone to get their attention
- Share photos, files, and updates
- Floating chat widget accessible from any portal page

### Calendar & Scheduling
- View your schedule (day/week/month)
- One-click Google Calendar link for new appointments
- See team availability to avoid conflicts
- Schedule inspections directly from lead detail

## SECTION 2: YOUR USE CASES — A Sales Rep's Day

### Morning (Before Heading Out)
1. Open sales portal on your phone → check dashboard
2. Review **Priority Leads** — who needs a call TODAY?
3. Check **Today's Inspections** — where are you going?
4. Tap **Map** on first inspection to get directions
5. Check **Quick Stats** — how close to monthly goal?
6. Scan **Recent Activity** — any new leads assigned overnight?

### On the Road
1. Use **Quick Call** to dial leads between stops
2. Update lead status after each call: New → Contacted
3. When scheduling an inspection: tap **Schedule** → pick date/time → Google Calendar link created
4. Use **Check My Address** at doors — show homeowner their hail report
5. After inspection: update status to "Inspected" + upload photos

### At an Inspection
1. Take photos with **Upload Photo** button
2. Pull up **Check My Address** report for the property
3. Show customer their risk data
4. If they're interested → update status to "Quoted"
5. Share **Customer Portal** link so they can track everything

### End of Day
1. Check **Performance Dashboard** — how did today go?
2. Update any remaining lead statuses
3. Check **GroupMe** for team messages
4. Review tomorrow's inspections
5. Check commission tracker — any pending deals close?

### Weekly Review
1. Check leaderboard ranking — moving up or down?
2. Review conversion rate — are you closing enough?
3. Check commission trends — on track for the month?
4. Review lead sources — where are your best leads coming from?

## SECTION 3: SETTINGS & CONFIGURATION GUIDE

### First-Time Login
1. Go to rivercityroofingsolutions.com/portal on your phone
2. Enter your @rcrsal.com email
3. Enter your PIN (provided by Sara/Michael)
4. You'll land on your Sales Dashboard

### Profile Setup
1. Go to My Profile (from dashboard or menu)
2. Upload a real photo of yourself (not AI-generated)
3. Update your bio — this shows on the public website
4. Verify your phone number and email
5. Save changes (may need admin approval)

### Lead Preferences
1. Go to Sales Portal → Settings (or Lead Preferences)
2. Set your preferred territory/area
3. Set notification preferences (how often, what types)
4. Save preferences

### Phone Setup
- Add your extension to your phone contacts for quick transfer
- Set up voicemail with a professional greeting
- Test that calls forward to your mobile correctly

## SECTION 4: HANDS-ON PRACTICE EXERCISES

### Exercise 1: Dashboard Tour (3 min)
1. Open /portal/sales on your phone
2. Read your Quick Stats — write down your Active Leads count
3. Check your commission progress bar — what percentage are you at?
4. Tap the Priority Leads section — how many urgent leads?
5. Scroll to Recent Activity — what's the latest entry?

### Exercise 2: Lead Management (5 min)
1. Go to /portal/sales/leads
2. Filter by "New" status
3. Pick a lead and tap to view details
4. Read the lead's contact info and source
5. Update the status from "New" to "Contacted"
6. Go back to the list — verify the status changed

### Exercise 3: Check My Address Demo (5 min)
1. Open /check-my-address on your phone
2. Enter your OWN home address
3. Fill in your name/email/phone
4. Submit and view your storm report
5. Note the risk score, hail events, and recommendations
6. Practice showing this to someone — "Here's what I found about your property..."

### Exercise 4: Customer CRM (5 min)
1. Go to /portal/sales/customers
2. Search for a customer name
3. Open the customer detail — explore all 6 tabs
4. Check the Documents tab — any files attached?
5. Check the Messages tab — any communication history?

### Exercise 5: Performance Check (3 min)
1. Go to /portal/sales/performance
2. Find your revenue this month
3. Find your conversion rate
4. Check your leaderboard position
5. Compare to last month — up or down?

### Exercise 6: Send a Customer Portal Link (3 min)
1. Find a customer in your leads or CRM
2. Look for the "Send Portal Link" button
3. Generate the portal link
4. Preview what the customer will see
5. Practice explaining: "I'm sending you a link to track your project..."

### Exercise 7: Team Chat (3 min)
1. Go to /portal/chat
2. Find the main team channel
3. Send a test message: "Testing the new system! 🏠"
4. Find a team member and send a DM
5. Try @mentioning someone
