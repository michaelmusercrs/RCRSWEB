## SECTION 1: SYSTEM FEATURES & FUNCTIONS

### Customer Records (JobNimbus CRM)
- Access customer records through /portal/sales/customers
- 6-tab customer detail view:
  1. Overview: Contact info, address, insurance company, policy details
  2. Active Jobs: Current projects with status
  3. Job History: Past completed work
  4. Documents: Contracts, estimates, inspection photos, adjuster reports, claim documents
  5. Messages: Full communication log with customer and team
  6. Transactions: Payments, insurance payouts, invoicing
- Two-way sync with JobNimbus — updates flow both directions

### Check My Address / Storm Data
- Access hail and storm reports for any address
- Use NWS data to support claims with historical storm events
- Risk scores, hail sizes, event dates — documented evidence
- Share reports with adjusters as supporting documentation

### Document Management
- Upload claim-related documents (adjuster reports, supplement requests, photos)
- Share documents with customers via their portal
- Access historical documents for repeat customers
- Organize by job/customer

### Customer Portal Sharing
- Share portal access with homeowners for transparency
- Customer can see: job timeline, documents, delivery tracking, messages
- Builds trust during the claims process
- Customer can track claim progress without calling the office

### Communication
- GroupMe chat with team
- Direct message sales reps about their customers' claims
- Coordinate with office on claim-related billing
- Monday Notes for claim status updates

### Lead/Customer Search
- Search across all customers and leads
- Filter by status, area, date
- Quick access to any customer's full record

### Schedule
- Schedule adjuster meetings
- Google Calendar links for appointments
- Coordinate inspection timing with sales reps

## SECTION 2: YOUR USE CASES

### Working a Claim
1. Sales rep closes a deal with insurance involvement
2. Bart gets assigned → opens customer detail in portal
3. Review: storm data (Check My Address), inspection photos, estimate
4. Prepare claim documentation
5. Upload all documents to customer record
6. Schedule adjuster meeting via calendar
7. Share customer portal with homeowner: "Track your claim here"
8. After adjuster visit: update job notes with adjuster's findings
9. If supplement needed: document and upload supplement request
10. Track claim through completion

### Supporting a Sales Rep
1. Rep calls about a potential insurance claim customer
2. Pull up Check My Address for the property
3. Review storm history and risk data
4. Advise rep on likelihood of successful claim
5. Provide talking points about insurance process

### Adjuster Coordination
1. Schedule meeting with adjuster via calendar
2. Prepare documentation package (photos, estimates, storm data)
3. Meet at property — review damage with adjuster
4. Document adjuster's findings in customer record
5. Negotiate if needed — reference storm data and documentation

### Customer Communication
1. Share customer portal link for transparency
2. Update messages in customer record
3. Upload new documents as they become available
4. Track customer inquiries and responses

## SECTION 4: HANDS-ON PRACTICE

### Exercise 1: Customer Lookup (5 min)
1. Go to /portal/sales/customers
2. Search for a customer
3. Open their 6-tab detail view
4. Review each tab: Overview, Active Jobs, History, Documents, Messages, Transactions

### Exercise 2: Storm Data Research (5 min)
1. Go to /check-my-address
2. Enter a customer's address (or test address)
3. Review the risk score and hail events
4. Think: How would you present this data to an adjuster?

### Exercise 3: Document Upload (5 min)
1. Open a customer record
2. Navigate to Documents tab
3. Upload a test document (any PDF or image)
4. Verify it appears in the document list

### Exercise 4: Schedule an Adjuster Meeting (5 min)
1. Go to /portal/schedule
2. Create a new event: "Adjuster Meeting - [Customer Name]"
3. Set date and time
4. Generate Google Calendar link
5. Verify it appears on the calendar

### Exercise 5: Share Customer Portal (3 min)
1. Find a customer record
2. Generate/view the customer portal link
3. Open the portal in a new tab — see what the customer sees
4. Practice explaining: "Here's where you can track your claim progress..."

## CLAIM WORKFLOW

```
Homeowner reports damage
  → Sales rep inspects → documents damage (photos)
  → Check My Address report pulled (storm data)
  → Bart assigned to claim
  → Documentation prepared (photos + storm data + estimate)
  → Adjuster meeting scheduled
  → Adjuster inspects → findings documented
  → Claim approved?
    → YES → Job proceeds → materials ordered → work completed → invoice → payment
    → PARTIAL → Supplement prepared → re-submitted → negotiate → resolve
    → DENIED → Review → appeal with additional documentation → re-inspect
  → Throughout: Customer tracks via portal → Bart updates status
```
