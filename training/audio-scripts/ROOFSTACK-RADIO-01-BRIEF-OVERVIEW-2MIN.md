## SCRIPT

Hey team, this is a quick overview of the new RCRS platform, and I promise it will only take two minutes.

Here is the big picture. We built one system that runs the entire business. No more scattered texts. No more lost spreadsheets. No more "I didn't get that message." Everything is in one place now, and it works on your phone, your tablet, your laptop -- whatever you have in front of you.

Let me walk you through the nine sections real quick.

First, the Public Website. Over three hundred pages of SEO content driving leads to us twenty-four seven -- service pages, blog articles, location pages, contact forms, all of it.

Second, Check My Address. Any homeowner types in their address and gets a free hail damage report with a risk score. It is a lead capture machine and the best door-knocking tool you have ever had.

Third, the Command Center. This is the executive dashboard -- real-time KPIs, revenue tracking, a team leaderboard with over two-point-six million dollars in tracked commissions, and insights on every part of the business.

Fourth, the Sales Portal. Mobile-first, built for the field. Your leads, your commissions, your schedule, your CRM -- all on your phone.

Fifth, the Office Portal. Material orders, delivery tickets, invoicing, scheduling -- the daily operations hub.

Sixth, the Delivery Portal. Routes, loading checklists, proof of delivery, and ETA notifications for customers.

Seventh, Inventory. Eleven products tracked in real time with low-stock alerts and a full audit trail.

Eighth, Billing. Invoices generated from job data, payment tracking, commission calculations, and aging reports.

And ninth, Training. Seven modules with quizzes, a seventy percent pass requirement, and completion certificates.

All of this is connected to the systems we already use -- Google Sheets, JobNimbus CRM, GroupMe for team chat, Google Calendar, TeamUp, live weather data from the National Weather Service, and Google Analytics tracking everything on the public site.

Every single one of you gets a personalized, role-specific dashboard at rivercityroofingsolutions.com/portal. Sales reps see leads and commissions. Drivers see routes and checklists. Managers see the full picture. One login. One place. Everything you need.

Training kicks off February seventeenth. If you have questions, talk to Michael.

Let's go.
