# AUDIO SCRIPT 02: The RCRS Platform -- Guided Tour

**Type:** Section-by-Section Walkthrough
**Duration:** ~10 minutes (~1,500 words)
**Tone:** Conversational, guided-tour narration, professional but approachable
**Audience:** Full RCRS team

---

## SCRIPT

Hey everyone. Welcome to the guided tour of the new RCRS platform. Over the next ten minutes, I am going to walk you through every major section of this system -- what it does, how it works, and why it matters for your role. Let's get into it.

### Section One: The Public Website

The public website at rivercityroofingsolutions.com is the twenty-four-seven face of River City Roofing Solutions. We are not talking about a simple five-page brochure site. This is over three hundred pages of SEO-optimized content -- sixty-eight published blog articles, seventeen individual team profile pages, detailed service pages for everything we offer, and dedicated location pages for Huntsville, Madison, Decatur, with expansion pages for Birmingham and Nashville.

Every page has structured data built in -- that is JSON-LD markup that tells Google exactly who we are, what we do, and where we serve. Our contact form drops leads directly into the pipeline through Google Sheets and triggers an email notification to the team. We have a referral program page with a rewards calculator, a BNI networking page for partner referrals, and Google Analytics tracking all of our traffic and conversions under ID G-Y8PB85BZC5.

Boston manages the content and analytics. Sales reps, your profile pages are live -- share those links with prospects. Chris and Michael, you can see lead volume and traffic trends anytime through the analytics dashboard.

### Section Two: Check My Address

Now let's talk about Check My Address. This is one of the most powerful tools in the platform. It lives at slash-check-my-address, and it is linked from the header, the footer, and the homepage.

Here is how it works. A homeowner enters their street address and contact information into a clean, simple form. Behind the scenes, the system pulls real historical data from the National Weather Service -- actual hail events, storm records, wind data for that specific area. It runs a risk scoring algorithm and generates a score from zero to one hundred, color-coded by severity. The homeowner sees a timeline of past hail events near their home, a clear risk level, and actionable recommendations.

But here is the part that matters for us: it automatically creates a lead. Name, email, phone, address, plus the full storm report -- all captured, all in the system. Sales reps, this is your door-knocking secret weapon. Pull up any address on your phone, show the homeowner their personalized storm report, point to the specific hail events near their property, and offer a free inspection right there.

### Section Three: The Command Center

Moving on to the Command Center. This is the executive dashboard -- the cockpit of the entire business. Owners and managers, this is your home base.

At the top, you have got KPI cards showing Revenue Month-to-Date, Revenue Year-to-Date, Gross Margin, Pipeline Value, and Cash Flow -- all updating in real time. Below that, a twelve-month revenue trend chart so you can see exactly where we are compared to where we have been.

The team leaderboard is here with over two-point-six million dollars in tracked commissions across four thousand one hundred and ninety-nine transactions. There is an insights panel that highlights trends and anomalies, financial reports with drill-down capability, and a full lead management section showing source tracking and conversion rates.

But the Command Center is more than just numbers. It has a marketing hub with ten ad variations, five email templates, and a content calendar. A meeting module where you can prep your Monday agenda, present it in slides mode, and archive past meeting notes. And a phone system overview managing eight extensions with call routing and voicemail.

### Section Four: The Sales Portal

Alright, sales team -- this one is for you. The Sales Portal is built mobile-first, because we know you are in the field, not sitting at a desk. Everything you need is designed to work from your phone while you are standing on a roof or sitting in your truck.

Your dashboard opens with a commission progress bar showing real-time earnings. Below that, quick stats -- deals closed, pipeline value, win rate -- and a team comparison card showing where you rank on the leaderboard. You have got a priority leads section with inline buttons to Call, Text, Send a Portal Link, or View Details for any lead, without leaving the page.

Quick-action buttons let you make a call, schedule an inspection, send a quote, or upload photos in one tap. Today's inspections show up with a map view for route planning. And at the bottom, a thumb-friendly mobile navigation bar keeps everything within reach.

The lead management system tracks every lead through a clear status workflow -- New, Contacted, Scheduled, Inspected, Quoted, Won, or Lost. Customer details are powered by JobNimbus with a six-tab view covering Overview, Active Jobs, History, Documents, Messages, and Transactions. And your performance dashboard keeps you in the loop on where you stand.

### Section Five: The Office Portal

Now let's talk about the Office Portal. Sara, Tia, Destin, John -- this is your workspace. It is built around a four-tab dashboard.

Tab one is your overview -- active delivery tickets, completed today, pending invoices, and the total pending dollar amount. Tab two is delivery ticket management with search, filtering, driver assignment, and the ability to pull materials directly from inventory. Tab three is invoice management where you can filter by status and mark invoices as paid. And tab four is the order creation form.

That order form is worth highlighting. You enter job info, customer contact details, project manager information, delivery date and time, priority level, and then you pick materials from a product grid with a running total at the bottom. When you submit, it creates the order, generates a delivery ticket, updates inventory, and notifies the assigned driver -- all in one step.

The Office Portal also handles scheduling, lead entry for walk-ins and phone calls, and phone operations across all eight extensions.

### Section Six: Delivery and Driver Portal

Moving on to deliveries. Richard, Tae, John -- this is your daily operations screen. It has two view modes: a list view and a map view showing all assigned deliveries.

The route management system gives each driver an ordered sequence of stops with progress bars, total distance, and estimated completion time. Every stop has a one-tap Google Maps navigation button, status indicators, priority flags, customer info, and action buttons to mark arrival, complete delivery, or capture proof.

Before a driver leaves, they go through the loading checklist. That means verifying every item against the ticket, confirming quantities, taking photos of loaded materials, running a safety check, and signing off digitally. Once the checklist is done, the ticket advances to Load Verified and the driver is clear to depart.

At each job site, proof of delivery is captured -- photos, notes, and a status update, all uploaded right there on-site. The ETA system automatically recalculates arrival times and sends customer notifications when the driver is en route.

### Section Seven: Inventory Management

Inventory tracks eleven products -- shingles, underlayment, pipe boots, ridge caps, nails, sealant, and more. Everything syncs in real time with Google Sheets as the data backend.

When stock drops below a configurable threshold, a low-stock alert fires. Every single stock movement is logged in the transaction history with who changed what, when, and why -- a full audit trail. And here is an important detail: cost visibility is role-based. Owners see the full dollar costs. Other roles see quantities only. That way everyone has the information they need without seeing data they don't.

### Section Eight: Billing and Invoicing

Billing turns completed jobs into invoices and tracks every dollar from creation through payment. Invoices are generated from job data with pre-filled customer and job details. They follow a clear status workflow -- Created, Sent, Paid, or Overdue.

You get customer aging reports that show outstanding balances at thirty, sixty, and ninety-plus days. Commission calculations are tied to closed deals, so when an invoice moves to Paid, the rep's commission updates automatically. Invoices can be exported as PDFs for email or print. And overdue alerts make sure nothing slips through the cracks.

### Section Nine: Training and Onboarding

The last section is Training, and it lives at slash-portal-slash-training with three learning paths.

Sales Training has seven modules covering everything from company overview to objection handling to customer communication. Each module ends with a quiz, and you need seventy percent to pass. When you complete all seven, you earn a certificate. Interface Onboarding walks you through eight sections of the platform with "Try It" links that open the actual tools so you learn by doing. And RCRS University is for ongoing professional development.

All progress is tracked in Google Sheets, so management can see who has completed what.

### How It All Connects

Everything ties together through our connected systems. Google Sheets is the data backbone. JobNimbus CRM syncs contacts, jobs, and notes in both directions. GroupMe Chat is built right into the portal for team messaging and direct conversations. Google Calendar and TeamUp handle scheduling. The National Weather Service feeds real storm data into Check My Address. And Google Analytics tracks everything on the public site.

The key workflow is simple: a lead comes in, a rep works it, we win the job, the office orders materials, a driver delivers them, we invoice the customer, payment comes in, and commissions are calculated. Every step of that process is visible, tracked, and connected in this single platform.
