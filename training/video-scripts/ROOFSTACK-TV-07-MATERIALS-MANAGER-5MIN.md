# VIDEO 07: Inventory Management -- Keeping RCRS Stocked

**Duration:** 5 minutes
**Audience:** Tae (Materials Manager)
**URL:** /portal/inventory

---

## NARRATION SCRIPT

[SCREEN: Browser opens /portal/inventory. The header shows "Inventory Management" with a package icon in green. Beneath the title it reads "142 items | 3 low stock" (the low stock count is in amber). A Refresh button and a green "Add Item" button are on the right side. Below the header, a stats bar shows Total Items, Total Value (formatted as currency), and a low stock warning with amber triangle icon.]

Welcome to Inventory Management. This is your dashboard for tracking every product RCRS stocks -- from shingles to nails to underlayment. When you open this page, the header tells you how many items are in the system and how many are below minimum stock level. The stats bar shows your Total Items count, the Total Value of all inventory in dollars, and a low stock warning if any items need attention.

[SCREEN: Three tabs below the stats bar -- "All Inventory" (package icon, active), "Low Stock" (warning triangle icon with an amber count badge), and "Transaction History" (clock/history icon).]

You have three tabs. All Inventory shows every product. Low Stock filters to only items below their minimum threshold -- this is where you should look first each morning. Transaction History shows every stock movement as an audit trail.

[SCREEN: Below the tabs, a search bar with placeholder "Search by name, SKU, supplier..." and a category filter dropdown showing "All Categories." Below, a list of inventory items appears. Each item row shows: a stock level progress bar on the left, the product name with a colored status badge (green "In Stock", amber "Low Stock", or red "Out of Stock"), SKU, category, location, and supplier underneath. On the right side: the current quantity in large bold text with the unit and minimum, and action buttons for Quick Adjust and Edit.]

The main inventory list shows every product. On the far left is a small progress bar that visually shows how full the stock is relative to the maximum. The product name is in bold white with a status badge next to it. Green "In Stock" means you are good. Amber "Low Stock" means the quantity has dropped to or below the minimum. Red "Out of Stock" means the quantity is zero. Below the product name you can see the SKU, category, warehouse location, and supplier.

On the right side, the current quantity is shown in large bold text with the unit type -- squares, bundles, rolls, boxes -- and the minimum quantity in parentheses. Two action buttons appear: the bar chart icon for Quick Adjust and the pencil icon for Edit.

Use the search bar to find products by name, SKU, or supplier. Use the category dropdown to filter by product type -- you will see categories like Shingles, Underlayment, Flashing, Ridge Vents, Fasteners, and more. There are 11 product categories total in the system.

[SCREEN: Click the "Low Stock" tab. The list filters to show only items with amber or red status badges. The tab shows a count badge next to it.]

Click the Low Stock tab first thing every morning. This shows only items that need reordering. If you see an amber "Low Stock" badge, it is time to place a reorder with the supplier. If you see red "Out of Stock," that is urgent -- crews may be waiting on that material.

[SCREEN: Click back to "All Inventory" tab. Click the expand arrow on a product row. The row expands to show a "Transaction History" section for that specific item, listing recent transactions with green up arrows for restocks and red down arrows for deliveries. Below the transactions, four detail cards show: Unit Cost, Total Value, Min/Max, and Location.]

Click the expand arrow on any product to see its individual transaction history. Each transaction shows whether stock went up (green arrow for restocks and returns) or down (red arrow for deliveries), the date and time, the transaction type, a reference number if applicable, and the quantity change. Below the transactions, four summary cards show the Unit Cost, Total Value of current stock, Min/Max thresholds, and warehouse Location.

[SCREEN: Click the bar chart Quick Adjust button on a product. A panel slides down showing an adjustment input with minus and plus buttons, a quantity field, a "Reason" text input, and green "Apply" and gray "Cancel" buttons. Below, a preview reads "New quantity: 47 each" in green or amber.]

To make a stock adjustment, click the Quick Adjust icon. A panel drops down with minus and plus buttons to increment the adjustment quantity, a text field for the reason -- for example, "Physical count" or "Restock from ABC Supply" -- and an Apply button. The system previews the new quantity before you confirm. If the new quantity will be at or below the minimum, it shows in amber as a warning. Click Apply to save the adjustment. This logs a transaction in the history automatically.

[SCREEN: Click the "Transaction History" tab. A table appears with columns: Date, Item, Type (colored badges for delivery, restock, return, adjustment), Qty Change (green positive, red negative), Reference number, Value, and Status (completed, pending, cancelled).]

The Transaction History tab shows a complete audit trail. Every stock movement is recorded here -- deliveries going out, restocks coming in, returns, and manual adjustments. The table shows the date, which item was affected, the transaction type with a color-coded badge, the quantity change in green or red, a reference number, the dollar value, and the status. This is your proof of record for every material movement.

[SCREEN: Click the green "Add Item" button in the header. A modal appears with fields: Product Name, Category, SKU, Initial Qty, Min Qty, Max Qty, Unit Cost, Unit (dropdown with options: each, box, bag, roll, piece, tube, bundle, pallet), Location, and Supplier.]

When a new product needs to be added to inventory, click the green "Add Item" button. A form pops up asking for the Product Name, Category, SKU, initial quantity, minimum and maximum thresholds, unit cost, unit type, warehouse location, and supplier name. Fill in the details and click "Add Item" to create it. The product appears in the inventory list immediately.

[SCREEN: Click the pencil Edit button on a product. A modal shows all editable fields: Product Name, Category, SKU, Current Qty, Min Qty, Max Qty, Unit Cost, Unit, Location, Supplier, and Notes. A green "Save Changes" button at the bottom.]

To edit an existing product -- for example, to update the supplier or adjust the reorder thresholds -- click the pencil Edit icon. A modal opens with all the product fields. Change what you need, then click Save Changes. Use this when a supplier changes pricing or when you want to adjust the minimum quantity to better match demand.

[SCREEN: The inventory page filtered to show a few low stock items with amber badges. The user performs a Quick Adjust on one, entering "+50" with the reason "Restock from ABC Supply."]

At the end of each day, do a quick review. Check the Low Stock tab. If deliveries went out today, verify the quantities are correct. If a restock arrived, do a Quick Adjust to add the new inventory. If you physically counted something and it does not match, adjust it and note "Physical count correction" as the reason. This keeps our records accurate and ensures crews always have what they need.

[SCREEN: The full inventory page, scrolled to the top showing the header, stats bar, and the beginning of the product list.]

That is Inventory Management. Check low stock every morning, process restocks with Quick Adjust, track everything in the transaction history, and coordinate with the driver on what gets loaded. You are the person who makes sure no crew ever shows up to a job without materials.
