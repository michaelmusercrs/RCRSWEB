# VIDEO 02: Sales Portal -- Your First Day

**Duration:** 5 minutes
**Audience:** Brendon, Greg, Travis, Hunter, Aaron (Sales Reps)
**URL:** /portal/sales
**Note:** This walkthrough is filmed on a phone. All screens are mobile-first.

---

## NARRATION SCRIPT

[SCREEN: Phone showing the RCRS Sales Portal at /portal/sales. The header has the RCRS logo, "Sales Portal" title, a refresh button, a notification bell, and the rep's name with a green "Sales Rep" badge. Below, a greeting reads "Hello, Brendon!" with "Let's close some deals today."]

Welcome to your Sales Portal. This is your mobile command center -- designed to work on your phone so you can use it in the truck, on a roof, or in a customer's driveway. When you log in, the app greets you by first name and shows your Sales Rep badge.

[SCREEN: Phone showing the Monthly Commission Progress bar. A blue progress bar shows "$12,400 / $100,000" with "12.4% complete" and "$87,600 to go" beneath it.]

The first thing you see is your Monthly Commission Progress bar. This tracks how much you have earned versus your monthly goal. The bar changes color as you get closer: blue when you are getting started, yellow-orange when you pass 75 percent, and green when you hit your goal. Below the bar it shows exactly how much you still need to close.

[SCREEN: Phone showing the four Quick Stats cards in a 2x2 grid -- Active Leads (blue person icon), Deals Closed (green checkmark), Rank (yellow trophy), and Commission (green dollar sign).]

Below that, four stat cards give you the quick picture. Active Leads is how many prospects are in your pipeline. Deals Closed is your count for the month. Rank shows where you stand on the team leaderboard. And Commission shows your total earned this month. These numbers update from the real sales data, not estimates.

[SCREEN: Phone showing the "vs Team Average" card with a green arrow pointing up and "+14.2% above average".]

If the system has enough data, you will see a "vs Team Average" card. This tells you if you are above or below the team average in revenue. Green with an up arrow means you are outperforming. Orange with a down arrow means it is time to pick up the pace. It also shows the team average dollar amount and average deal count so you know the benchmark.

[SCREEN: Phone showing the "My Commissions" section with three cards -- Earned (green), Pending (yellow), and Avg Deal (white).]

The My Commissions section breaks your money into three boxes: Earned is what has already been paid, Pending is commission on deals that have not been paid out yet, and Avg Deal shows your average deal size so you can track whether you are going after bigger jobs.

[SCREEN: Phone showing the Quick Actions row -- four round buttons labeled Quick Call (green phone), Schedule (blue calendar), Send Quote (purple document), and Upload Photo (orange camera).]

These four Quick Action buttons are your most-used tools. Quick Call opens a list of your leads so you can tap to call in one touch. Schedule takes you to the leads page with the scheduling filter. Send Quote opens the quoting flow. And Upload Photo lets you snap an inspection photo right from the field.

[SCREEN: Phone showing the Priority Leads section. Two lead cards appear with customer name, status badge, address, priority label, and estimated value. Each card has four action buttons: Call (green), Text (blue), Portal (purple), and Details (gray arrow).]

Priority Leads shows your highest-priority prospects -- the ones marked urgent or high. Each card shows the customer name, their current status like "New" or "Contacted," the property address, the priority level, and the estimated deal value. Beneath each lead are four buttons. Call dials them immediately. Text opens a text message. Portal sends them their customer portal link via text so they can track their own project. And Details takes you to the full customer record.

[SCREEN: Phone showing the Today's Inspections section. A card with a cyan border shows a time, customer name, address, and a map pin button on the right.]

Today's Inspections shows any appointments you have scheduled for today. You see the time, the customer name, and the address. Tap the map pin button on the right and it opens Google Maps with turn-by-turn directions to the property. If no inspections are scheduled, you will see a "Schedule an inspection" link.

[SCREEN: Phone tapping into /portal/sales/leads. A full list of leads with status badges, search bar, and filters.]

Tap "All Leads" or the Leads icon in the bottom navigation to see your full lead list. You can search by name and filter by status. Each lead shows their name, status badge, address, priority, and estimated value. Tap any lead to open their detail page where you can update the status -- for example, changing a lead from "New" to "Contacted" after your first call.

[SCREEN: Phone showing /check-my-address. A form with address fields and a big green "Get My Free Storm Report" button.]

One of your best prospecting tools is Check My Address. Go to /check-my-address or tell a homeowner to visit our website and click "Check My Address" in the main menu. They enter their address and get a free storm report showing hail events near their property, a risk score, and a recommendation. When they submit it, a lead is automatically created and assigned to the team. You can use this on the doorstep: pull it up on your phone, type in their address, and show them the report right there.

[SCREEN: Phone showing /portal/sales/performance. Charts and stats for the rep's personal performance.]

Tap "Performance" or the Stats icon in the bottom navigation to see your personal performance page. This shows your revenue over time, deal history, and how you compare to the rest of the team. Use this to track your own progress and set personal goals.

[SCREEN: Phone showing the bottom navigation bar with five icons -- Home, Leads, a green circular Phone button in the center, Stats, and Portal.]

At the very bottom of your screen is the navigation bar. Home brings you back to this dashboard. Leads shows your full lead list. The big green phone button in the center is your quick call shortcut -- tap it anytime to make a call. Stats is your performance page. And Portal takes you to the main company portal.

[SCREEN: Phone showing the GroupMe chat at /portal/chat. A message thread with team members.]

One more thing -- the GroupMe chat. Tap the chat icon from the portal to send messages to the whole team or DM an individual. Use this to coordinate with the office, ask the PM about material orders, or share a win with the team.

[SCREEN: Phone showing the Sales Portal dashboard, scrolled to the top.]

That is your Sales Portal. Commission progress, quick stats, priority leads, one-tap calling, scheduled inspections, and the Check My Address tool. Keep this open on your phone all day. The more you use it, the more deals you will close.
