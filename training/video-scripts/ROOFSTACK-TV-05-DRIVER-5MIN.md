# VIDEO 05: Driver Portal -- Your Delivery Day

**Duration:** 5 minutes
**Audience:** Richard (Driver)
**URL:** /portal/driver
**Note:** This walkthrough is filmed on a phone. All screens are mobile-first.

---

## NARRATION SCRIPT

[SCREEN: Phone browser opening rivercityroofingsolutions.com/portal. The login page appears with the RCRS logo and fields for email and PIN.]

Welcome to the RCRS Driver Portal. This is your delivery command center -- everything you need for the day lives right here on your phone. Start by going to rivercityroofingsolutions.com/portal on your phone's browser. Enter your email and PIN to log in. Once you are in, you will see your dashboard with four tiles: Monday Notes, Chat, My Profile, and My Deliveries. Tap "My Deliveries" to get started with your day.

[SCREEN: Phone showing /portal/driver. The driver portal loads with a summary bar at the top: Active Routes, Total Stops, Completed, Remaining, and Unassigned. Below, a list of delivery stop cards for the day, each with a job name, customer name, address, priority badge, and status indicator.]

This is your Driver Portal. At the top you see your daily summary: how many active routes you have, total stops for the day, how many you have completed, how many are remaining, and whether any tickets are unassigned. Below that is your full list of deliveries. Each card shows the job name, the customer name, the delivery address, a priority badge -- Normal, Rush, or Urgent -- and a status indicator. Urgent and Rush deliveries are highlighted so you never miss them. Scan this list first thing every morning to plan your day.

[SCREEN: Phone tapping the "Full Route" button at the top. Google Maps opens with all delivery stops plotted as waypoints in order, showing total distance and estimated drive time.]

Tap "Full Route" at the top of your delivery list and Google Maps opens with every stop mapped in order. You can see the total distance and estimated time for the entire route. This helps you understand the full scope of the day before you leave the warehouse. Close Maps and head back to the portal when you are ready to start loading.

[SCREEN: Phone navigating to /portal/driver/loading. The Loading Checklist page appears with a header reading "Loading Checklist" and a list of material items grouped by delivery. Each item has a checkbox, product name, quantity, and the delivery it belongs to. At the bottom: a photo upload area, safety checks, and a "Sign Off" button.]

Before you leave the warehouse, you must complete the Loading Checklist. Go to /portal/driver/loading or tap "Loading Checklist" from your driver portal. This page lists every material you need to load, grouped by delivery. For each item, you see the product name and the quantity needed. As you pull each item and load it onto the truck, tap the checkbox to mark it verified. Load in route order -- the last delivery goes on the bottom of the truck, and the first delivery goes on top so you can grab it first. Once all items are checked off, scroll down to the photo section. Take a photo of the loaded truck so we have a record. Then complete the safety checks: truck condition, load secured, all materials accounted for. Finally, tap "Sign Off" to confirm everything is ready. You cannot start your route until this checklist is complete.

[SCREEN: Phone back on /portal/driver. The first delivery stop card is expanded, showing full detail: stop number 1, status "Planned," customer name and phone number, full address, item count, scheduled time, special instructions reading "Gate code: 4521 -- stack materials by garage," and four action buttons at the bottom: Navigate, Details, Start Delivery, and Photos.]

Now you are on the road. Look at your first stop. Each delivery card shows everything you need: the stop number, the customer name with a tappable phone number in case you need to call them, the full address, how many items are on this delivery, the scheduled time, and any special instructions. Always read special instructions before you arrive -- gate codes, where to stack materials, whether an inspector needs to be present. The four buttons at the bottom are your action tools. Tap "Navigate" and Google Maps opens with turn-by-turn directions to that specific stop. Tap "Details" to see the full material list for this delivery.

[SCREEN: Phone tapping "Start Delivery" on the first stop card. The status changes from "Planned" to "In Progress" with a blue indicator. A timestamp appears: "Started: 8:47 AM." The progress bar at the top updates. An ETA notification banner briefly flashes: "Customer notified -- ETA updated."]

When you arrive at the delivery site, tap "Start Delivery." The status changes to "In Progress" and a timestamp is recorded. The system automatically sends an ETA notification to the customer and the office so they know you have arrived. As you move between stops throughout the day, the ETA auto-adjusts -- if you are running early or late, the system recalculates and keeps everyone informed without you having to make phone calls.

[SCREEN: Phone tapping the "Photos" button. The camera opens. A photo is taken of roofing materials stacked neatly beside a garage. The photo appears as a thumbnail with an "Add Note" text field below it. The note reads: "Materials stacked by garage per instructions. All items delivered." Below that, a green "Complete Delivery" button.]

After you unload, tap "Photos" to open your camera and take a picture of the delivered materials at the site. This is your proof of delivery. Take a clear photo that shows what was delivered and where it was placed. You can take multiple photos if needed. Below the photo, add a delivery note -- for example, "Materials stacked by garage per instructions. All items delivered." If there were any issues, note them here too. When everything is documented, tap "Complete Delivery."

[SCREEN: The stop card now shows status "Delivered" with a green checkmark, a completion timestamp "Completed: 9:12 AM," and the delivery photos attached. A notification banner briefly appears: "Customer and office notified." The next stop card is now highlighted as the active delivery.]

The delivery is now marked complete with a green checkmark and the exact time recorded. The system automatically sends a notification to the customer and the office that the delivery has been made. Your photos and notes are saved to the delivery record. The progress bar at the top updates, and the next stop on your route is now highlighted and ready to go. Tap "Navigate" on the next stop and keep moving through your route.

[SCREEN: Phone showing the GroupMe chat at /portal/chat. A message thread with team members. A message from Richard reads: "Running 15 min behind on stop 3 -- traffic on 565." A reply from Sara reads: "Got it, I'll let the customer know."]

If anything comes up during the day, use GroupMe chat. Tap "Chat" from the portal to message the office or any team member. Running late? Send a quick message so the office can notify the customer. Wrong materials on the list? Message the office immediately -- do not deliver the wrong materials. Need to ask the PM about a special request? Send a DM. The chat keeps everyone in the loop without phone calls back and forth.

[SCREEN: Phone showing the driver portal at end of day. All stops show green "Delivered" checkmarks. The progress bar reads "5/5 Complete." The summary shows: Completed 5, Remaining 0.]

At the end of the day, your portal should show all green checkmarks. Every stop completed, every delivery documented with photos and notes. If any stop could not be completed, its status will still show as Planned or In Progress -- message the office with the reason so they can reschedule. Before you leave, check if tomorrow's route is already loaded. If it is, take a quick look at the stops so you know what to expect in the morning.

[SCREEN: Phone showing the driver portal dashboard, scrolled to the top.]

That is your complete delivery workflow. Check your route, complete the loading checklist, navigate to each stop, start the delivery, take photos, add notes, mark it complete, and move to the next stop. Keep this portal open on your phone all day. It replaces clipboards, phone calls, and guesswork with one simple system that keeps you, the office, and the customer all on the same page.
