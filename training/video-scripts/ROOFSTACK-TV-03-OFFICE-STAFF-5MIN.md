# VIDEO 03: Office Portal -- Daily Operations Mastery

**Duration:** 5 minutes
**Audience:** Sara Hill, Tia, Destin (Office Staff)
**URL:** /portal/office

---

## NARRATION SCRIPT

[SCREEN: Browser opens /portal/office. The header shows "Office Portal" with "Job Tracking & Invoicing" subtitle, a Command Center shortcut button in green, and a Refresh button. Below the header, four tabs are visible: Dashboard (building icon), Delivery Tickets (package icon), Invoices (dollar icon), and New Order (plus icon).]

Welcome to the Office Portal. This is where you manage delivery tickets, track invoices, and create new material orders. Everything the office needs to keep jobs moving lives right here.

[SCREEN: The Dashboard tab is active. Four stat cards are displayed in a grid -- Active Deliveries (blue truck icon), Completed Today (green checkmark icon), Pending Invoices (yellow document icon), and Pending Amount (green dollar icon showing a dollar amount).]

When you first open the portal, you land on the Dashboard tab. Four stat cards across the top give you the day's snapshot. Active Deliveries shows how many tickets are in progress right now. Completed Today shows how many have been finished. Pending Invoices is the count of invoices waiting to be paid. And Pending Amount shows the total dollar value outstanding. These numbers update automatically every time you refresh.

[SCREEN: Scroll down on the Dashboard tab. Two sections appear -- "Recent Tickets" showing the five most recent delivery tickets with colored status dots, job names, customer names, and dates. Below that, "Recent Invoices" showing five invoices with job name, invoice ID, dollar amount, and a status badge (paid in green, pending in yellow).]

Below the stat cards, the Dashboard shows your five most recent tickets and five most recent invoices. Each ticket has a colored status dot -- gray for created, cyan for assigned, yellow for materials pulled, purple for en route, green for completed. Each invoice shows the job name, invoice number, total amount, and a status badge. Click "View All" on either section to jump to the full list.

[SCREEN: Click the "Delivery Tickets" tab. A search bar and a status filter dropdown appear at the top. Below that, a table with columns: Ticket (job name and ticket ID), Customer (name and phone), Status (colored badge), Driver (name or an "Assign" dropdown), Amount, and Actions.]

Click the Delivery Tickets tab. At the top you have a search bar -- type any job name, customer name, or ticket ID to filter. Next to it is a status dropdown where you can filter by Created, Materials Pulled, Load Verified, En Route, Arrived, Delivered, or Completed.

The table shows every ticket. The Ticket column shows the job name and the ticket ID. Customer shows the name and phone number. Status shows a colored badge. The Driver column is important -- if a ticket has been assigned, the driver name appears here. If the ticket status is "Created" and no driver is assigned yet, you will see a dropdown that says "Assign." Click that dropdown and select an available driver to assign the delivery.

[SCREEN: In the Driver column, a dropdown opens showing "Richard - Available." Select Richard. The page refreshes and the ticket now shows "Richard" as the assigned driver.]

Here I am assigning Richard to this delivery. Once I select him from the dropdown, the system assigns the ticket and Richard will see it on his driver portal. If materials still need to be pulled, you will see a "Pull Materials" button in the Actions column -- click that to advance the ticket to the materials pulled stage.

[SCREEN: Click the "Invoices" tab. A search bar appears at the top. Below it, a table shows columns: Invoice (ID and job name), Customer, Date, Due date, Amount (with materials subtotal), Status (colored badge), and Actions.]

Now click the Invoices tab. Again, you can search by job name, customer name, or invoice number. The table shows every invoice with its ID, customer, creation date, due date, total amount with the materials subtotal beneath it, and status. Statuses are color-coded: green for paid, yellow for pending, blue for sent, and red for overdue.

[SCREEN: In the Actions column of a pending invoice, click the "Mark Paid" button that shows a credit card icon. The status badge changes from yellow "pending" to green "paid."]

When you receive a payment, find the invoice and click "Mark Paid" in the Actions column. It has a credit card icon. The system records the payment and changes the status to paid. That is all it takes.

[SCREEN: Click the "New Order" tab (labeled "Create Order" with a plus icon). A form appears with sections: Job Information, Project Manager, Delivery Details, and Materials. The Job Information section has fields for Job Name, Customer Name, Address, City, State, ZIP, Customer Phone, and Customer Email.]

The New Order tab is where you create material orders that generate delivery tickets. Start at the top with Job Information. Enter the Job Name -- for example, "Smith Residence Roof." Then the Customer Name, full address, city, state, ZIP, phone, and email. Fields marked with an asterisk are required.

[SCREEN: Scroll down to the Project Manager section with fields for Name, Phone, and Email. Then the Delivery Details section with Requested Date, Preferred Time, Priority dropdown (Normal, Rush +$50, Urgent +$100), and Special Instructions textarea.]

Next, fill in the Project Manager -- that is usually John. Enter his name, phone, and email. Then set the Delivery Details: the requested delivery date, preferred time, and priority level. Normal is standard. Rush adds 50 dollars. Urgent adds 100 dollars. Use the Special Instructions field for gate codes, preferred drop location, or anything the driver needs to know.

[SCREEN: Scroll to the Materials section. A grid of product cards shows product names, categories, and prices. Clicking a product adds it to the "Selected Materials" list above, which shows quantity, unit price, total, and a running total at the bottom in a green highlighted bar.]

The Materials section is the heart of the order. You see a grid of available products from inventory, each showing the name, category, and price per unit. Click a product to add one unit. Once added, it appears in the "Selected Materials" list above the grid with the quantity, unit price, and line total. At the bottom, a green bar shows the running Total Materials cost. If you need to remove an item, click the red icon next to it.

[SCREEN: Click the green "Create Delivery Ticket" button at the bottom. The form submits and the page switches to the Delivery Tickets tab showing the new ticket at the top of the list.]

When everything is filled in, hit the green "Create Delivery Ticket" button. The system creates the ticket, and you are automatically switched to the Delivery Tickets tab where your new order appears at the top. From here you can assign a driver and the workflow begins.

[SCREEN: Navigate to /portal/schedule. A calendar view loads with events.]

For scheduling, go to the Schedule page from the portal menu. This shows all upcoming jobs, deliveries, and meetings in a calendar view. You can switch between month, week, and day views.

[SCREEN: Navigate to /portal/chat. The GroupMe chat interface shows a team conversation.]

Finally, use GroupMe chat to stay in touch with the team. If a driver calls in with a question, if a sales rep needs a delivery date, or if the PM changes an order -- communicate it here so everyone stays in the loop.

[SCREEN: Back on the Office Portal dashboard, zoomed out to show the full page.]

That is the Office Portal. Dashboard for the daily snapshot, Delivery Tickets for tracking and assigning, Invoices for payment management, and New Order for creating material deliveries. Master these four tabs and you are running the operation.
