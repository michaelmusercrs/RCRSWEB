# VIDEO 05: Driver Portal -- Everything You Need to Know

**Duration:** 3 minutes
**Audience:** Richard (Driver)
**URL:** /portal/driver
**Note:** This walkthrough is filmed on a phone. Simple language.

---

## NARRATION SCRIPT

[SCREEN: Phone showing the Driver Portal at /portal/driver. A bright green header bar shows the driver's name "Richard" with the vehicle name. Below is a route summary bar with three numbers: Total Stops, Completed, and Remaining. A green progress bar shows how far through the route you are.]

This is your Driver Portal. Open it on your phone every morning. The green bar at the top shows your name and your truck. Below that you see three numbers: Total Stops for the day, how many you have Completed, and how many are Remaining. The green progress bar fills up as you finish deliveries.

[SCREEN: Phone showing "Today's Route" with the current date. A list of delivery cards appears. Each card shows a numbered circle, the job name, customer name, address, scheduled time, number of items, and a status badge. Below each card are two quick buttons: "Navigate" (blue) and "Call" (green).]

Scroll down and you see your full route for the day. Each stop is a card with a number showing the order. You see the job name, the customer name, the address, what time it is scheduled, and how many items are on the truck. At the bottom of each card are two buttons. Navigate opens Google Maps with directions. Call dials the customer. If there are special instructions like a gate code, you will see a yellow "Instructions" label.

[SCREEN: Phone navigating to /portal/driver/loading. A loading checklist page shows delivery orders with item lists. Each item has a checkbox.]

Before you leave the warehouse, go to the Loading Checklist. This page shows every order for the day with all the materials listed. As you load each item onto the truck, tap the checkbox to mark it loaded. This makes sure nothing gets left behind. Go through every item on every order. When all items are checked, you are good to go.

[SCREEN: Phone back on the route list. Tap a delivery card. The detail view loads showing the job name, ticket ID, and status badge at the top. Below that, a horizontal progress strip shows the workflow steps: Verify Load, Start Delivery, Mark Arrived, Complete Delivery, Capture Proof, QC Photos, Complete.]

Tap any stop to open the detail view. At the top you see the job name and current status. Below that is the progress strip showing every step in the delivery. Green steps are done. The bright step is where you are now.

[SCREEN: Phone showing the detail view. A blue "Navigate to Stop" button with a compass icon. Above it, the delivery address and city.]

The delivery address is shown with a big blue "Navigate to Stop" button. Tap that button and Google Maps opens with directions from your current location.

[SCREEN: Phone showing the customer info card with a green "Tap to Call" button showing the phone number.]

If you need to reach the customer -- for example, nobody is home or you cannot find the house -- tap the green "Tap to Call" button. It dials their number directly.

[SCREEN: Phone showing the materials list for the stop -- product names, SKUs, quantities, and units. Below that, a yellow "Special Instructions" box with text.]

The Materials section lists everything that goes to this stop. Check it against what is on the truck. If there are Special Instructions, they show in a yellow box -- read these before you unload.

[SCREEN: Phone showing the bottom of the detail view. A big green button reads "Start Delivery" with a truck icon.]

At the bottom of the screen is your action button. It changes based on where you are in the workflow. Here is how the steps go:

When you are at the warehouse with materials loaded, tap "Verify Load." When you are leaving the warehouse, tap "Start Delivery." When you arrive at the job site, tap "Mark Arrived." After you unload everything, add any delivery notes in the text box -- like "left materials in driveway" -- then tap "Complete Delivery." Next, the system asks you to capture proof of delivery: enter the name of the person who accepted the materials and tap confirm. Finally, take QC photos if needed, and tap "Complete" to finish the stop.

[SCREEN: Phone showing the delivery notes textarea and a "Report an Issue" toggle with a yellow warning icon.]

If anything goes wrong -- damaged materials, wrong address, customer refused delivery -- tap "Report an Issue" under the delivery notes. Type what happened. This gets flagged for the office to review.

[SCREEN: Phone showing the route list with one stop now in the "Completed" section at the bottom, grayed out with a green checkmark.]

After completing a stop, it moves to the Completed section at the bottom of your route list, grayed out with a green checkmark. Move on to the next stop.

[SCREEN: Phone showing the route list at end of day. All stops are in the Completed section. The progress bar is 100% full.]

At the end of the day, all your stops should be in the Completed section and the progress bar should be full. If any stops could not be completed, make sure you reported the issue so the office knows.

[SCREEN: Phone showing the Driver Portal top-level view, scrolled to the top.]

That is everything. Check your route, use the loading checklist, navigate to each stop, deliver the materials, mark each step complete, and report any issues. Keep it simple and you will have a great day.
