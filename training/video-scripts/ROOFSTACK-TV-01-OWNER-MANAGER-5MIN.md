# VIDEO 01: Owner Dashboard -- Your Morning Routine

**Duration:** 5 minutes
**Audience:** Chris Muse, Michael Muse (Owner / Admin roles)
**URL:** /command-center

---

## NARRATION SCRIPT

[SCREEN: Browser opening rivercityroofingsolutions.com/command-center. The page loads with a dark themed dashboard. A greeting reads "Good morning, Chris!" with an "Owner" role badge in the top right.]

Welcome to the RCRS Command Center. This is your executive overview -- the first thing you should check every morning. When you log in, the system greets you by name and shows your role badge. Everything on this page updates in real time. If you ever want fresh numbers, hit the Refresh button in the top right corner.

[SCREEN: Camera zooms into the top row of five KPI cards -- Revenue MTD, Revenue YTD, Gross Margin, Pipeline Value, and Cash Flow. Each card shows a dollar amount, a trend arrow, and a percentage.]

Right at the top you have your five executive KPI cards. Revenue MTD shows what we have brought in this month, with the percentage change versus last month -- green arrow up is good, red arrow down means we need to push harder. Revenue YTD is the running total for the year. Gross Margin tells you whether we are staying above the 25 percent healthy threshold -- if it drops below, the card turns orange as a warning. Pipeline Value shows how much business is in the funnel that has not closed yet. And Cash Flow on the far right tracks net cash for the month -- positive shows green, negative shows red. Click any of these cards to jump straight into the full Financial Report.

[SCREEN: Scroll down to the second row of four stat cards -- Today's Sales, Active Jobs, Low Stock Alerts, and Team Active.]

Below the financials you have four operational stat cards that everyone on the admin team can see. Today's Sales, the number of Active Jobs this week, Low Stock Alerts that need attention in the warehouse, and how many Team members are active today. If Low Stock shows a number greater than zero, it turns orange so you notice it immediately.

[SCREEN: Scroll to the Revenue Trend section. A 12-month bar chart fills the left two-thirds of the screen, with green bars for each month. Below the chart, three summary numbers: This Month, Last Month, and 12-Month Total. To the right is the Insights panel with color-coded cards.]

This is your Revenue Trend chart -- twelve months of commission data displayed as a bar chart. Hover over any bar to see the exact dollar amount for that month. Below the chart you get a quick comparison: this month versus last month, and your rolling 12-month total in green. On the right side is the Insights panel. The system automatically generates tips and alerts: green cards are positive trends, orange cards are warnings, blue cards are informational, and purple cards are action tips. Scan these each morning for anything that needs your attention.

[SCREEN: Scroll to the Team Performance table. A table shows columns for Rank, Rep name with avatar, Revenue, Deals, Avg Deal, Percent of Total with a progress bar, and Status. The number one rep has a gold rank number and a flame icon labeled "Top".]

The Team Performance table is your leaderboard. Every sales rep is ranked by revenue for the current month. You can see their total revenue in green, how many deals they closed, their average deal size, and what percentage of total company revenue they represent. The top three reps get highlighted -- gold for first, silver for second, bronze for third -- and they show a flame icon with the label "Top." If anyone is below five percent, they get an orange warning flag. Click any rep name to drill into their individual detail page.

[SCREEN: Scroll to the Financial Alerts section. Two or three colored alert cards appear -- a red "critical" card and an orange "warning" card, each with a title, description, and dollar amount.]

If there are overdue invoices or cash flow concerns, they show up in the Financial Alerts section. Red means critical, orange means warning, blue means informational. Each alert shows the title, a description of the issue, and the dollar amount involved. Click "View all" to go to the full financial report.

[SCREEN: Scroll to Today's Schedule. A list of events with clock icons, event names, and colored type badges -- "meeting" in blue, "job" in green, "delivery" in orange.]

Today's Schedule shows everything on the calendar for the day. Each event has a time, title, and a color-coded badge: blue for meetings, green for jobs, purple for calls, orange for deliveries. Click "View full schedule" to see the calendar page with month, week, and day views.

[SCREEN: Click the "Sales Leaderboard" quick action card. The /command-center/leads page loads showing KPI stats, lead distribution, and an unassigned leads queue.]

Now let me show you two quick detours for your Monday morning prep. First, click into the Lead Dashboard. This shows total leads, conversion rate, average response time, and how many leads came in today. You can see which reps have leads assigned, who is responding fastest, and whether any leads are sitting unassigned. Use the Quick Assign button to route a lead to a rep right from this screen.

[SCREEN: Navigate to /command-center/meetings/prep. A meeting preparation page loads.]

Second, before your Monday meeting, go to Meeting Prep. This page pulls together everything you need to run your weekly team meeting -- performance summaries, action items, and talking points. You can present directly from this page or use the presentation view.

[SCREEN: Back on the main Command Center. The Quick Links footer shows four cards -- Billing, Breakdowns, Financials, and Team -- in a row at the bottom.]

Finally, at the very bottom of the dashboard you have Quick Links: Billing for invoices, Breakdowns for job-level cost detail, Financials for the full revenue and margin report, and Team for managing your roster. These are always one click away.

[SCREEN: The full Command Center dashboard in its final state, scrolled to the top.]

That is your morning routine. Log in, scan the five KPI cards, check the revenue trend, review the leaderboard, glance at alerts and today's schedule, and peek at leads. Five minutes and you know exactly where the business stands. Do this every morning and nothing will surprise you.
