# VIDEO 04: PM Portal -- Ordering Materials & Coordinating Deliveries

**Duration:** 5 minutes
**Audience:** John (Production Manager)
**URL:** /portal/pm

---

## NARRATION SCRIPT

[SCREEN: Browser opens /portal/pm. The header shows "Project Manager Portal" with "Welcome, John" underneath. A green "New Order" button is in the top right. Below, four tabs: Dashboard (package icon), Create Order (plus icon), My Tickets (truck icon), and Returns/Pickups (rotate icon).]

Welcome to the Project Manager Portal. This is where you create material orders, track deliveries, manage returns, and make sure every job site has what it needs. Let me walk you through everything.

[SCREEN: The Dashboard tab is active showing four stat cards -- Pending (yellow clock icon), In Progress (blue truck icon), Completed (green checkmark icon), and Total Orders (purple document icon). Each shows a number.]

When you log in, the Dashboard shows four stats. Pending is orders that have been submitted but have not left the warehouse yet. In Progress is orders that are currently being loaded or are on the road. Completed is finished deliveries. And Total Orders is your all-time count. Below the stats is a list of your Recent Orders with the job name, address, ticket type icon, status badge, cost, and the assigned driver name.

[SCREEN: Click the "Create Order" tab. A split-screen layout appears. On the left is the order form with sections for Order Type, Job Info, Customer Info, Date and Priority, and Special Instructions. On the right is the material selection panel with a search bar and a scrollable list of products.]

Click the Create Order tab and this is where you spend most of your time. The screen splits into two panels. On the left is the order form. On the right is the materials catalog.

[SCREEN: Focus on the Order Type section at the top of the left panel. Three buttons in a row: Delivery (blue truck), Pickup (orange package), and Return (purple rotate). Delivery is selected with a blue border.]

First, choose the Order Type. Delivery is the default -- that sends materials to a job site. Pickup brings materials from a job site back to the warehouse. Return sends materials back to the supplier. Click the one you need.

[SCREEN: Focus on the Job Info section. Fields show Job Name, Job ID, Address, City, State, ZIP, Customer Name, and Customer Phone.]

Fill in the Job Info. Enter the Job Name -- this should match how we reference the job. Job ID is optional but helpful for tracking. Then the full delivery address: street, city, state, and ZIP. Add the customer name and phone so the driver can contact them if needed.

[SCREEN: Focus on the Date and Priority section. Three fields: Date (a date picker), Time (a time picker), and Priority (a dropdown showing Normal, Rush +$50, Urgent +$100).]

Set the delivery date and preferred time. If the crew needs materials first thing in the morning, set the time for 7 AM. The Priority dropdown has three options: Normal for standard scheduling, Rush for an extra 50 dollars when you need it faster, and Urgent for 100 dollars when it absolutely must arrive today. Only use Urgent when the crew is waiting on materials and cannot work without them.

[SCREEN: Focus on the Special Instructions textarea.]

The Special Instructions field is critical. Put gate codes, where to drop the materials on the property, whether the homeowner has dogs in the yard, narrow driveway warnings -- anything the driver needs to know before arriving.

[SCREEN: Focus on the right panel -- Material Selection. A search bar at the top, then a scrollable list of products. Each product shows the name, category, SKU, retail price per unit, and current stock quantity. Below each product are four quantity buttons: +1, +5, +10, +25.]

Now the materials. The right panel shows every item in our inventory. Use the search bar at the top to find what you need by name, category, or SKU. Each product shows the name, category and SKU, the retail price per unit, and how many units are in stock. Below each item are quick-add buttons: plus 1, plus 5, plus 10, or plus 25. Tap the quantity you want. If stock is too low for that quantity, the button is grayed out.

[SCREEN: Click +10 on "Architectural Shingles" and +5 on "Drip Edge." The Selected Materials summary appears on the left below the form, showing each item with quantity, unit price, total, and a running sum at the bottom in green.]

As you add materials, they stack up in the Selected Materials summary on the left side. Here I have added 10 bundles of Architectural Shingles and 5 pieces of Drip Edge. Each line shows the quantity, the unit price, and the line total. The running total at the bottom is highlighted in green. If you added something by mistake, click the red X next to it to remove it.

[SCREEN: Click the green "Create Delivery Ticket" button at the bottom of the form. A success alert appears. The page switches to the My Tickets tab.]

When the order is ready, hit the green Create Delivery Ticket button. The system creates the ticket and confirms with a success message. You are automatically taken to the My Tickets tab.

[SCREEN: The My Tickets tab shows a list of delivery tickets. Each row has a blue truck icon, job name, address with map pin, scheduled date with calendar icon, status badge, cost, and assigned driver name.]

My Tickets shows all your delivery orders. Each ticket shows the job name, address, scheduled date, current status, total cost, and which driver is assigned. Statuses progress through created, assigned, materials pulled, load verified, en route, arrived, delivered, and completed. You can track exactly where every order is in the workflow.

[SCREEN: Navigate to /portal/inventory. The inventory page loads showing all products with stock levels, search, category filters, and colored stock status badges.]

To check stock levels before placing an order, go to the Inventory page. You can see every product with its current quantity, minimum threshold, and a stock status badge -- green for In Stock, amber for Low Stock, red for Out of Stock. Use this to verify availability before you promise materials to a job.

[SCREEN: Navigate to /portal/schedule. A calendar showing jobs, deliveries, and inspections.]

The Schedule page shows the full calendar. You can see all jobs, deliveries, and inspections for the week or month. Use this to coordinate delivery timing with when crews will be on site.

[SCREEN: Navigate to /portal/chat. A GroupMe message thread shows a conversation between John, the driver, and the office.]

GroupMe chat is your coordination tool. When you create an order, message the driver to let them know what is coming. If the office has questions about an order, they will reach out here. If a crew chief calls you and says they need something urgently, post it in the group chat so the warehouse and driver can see it simultaneously.

[SCREEN: Back on the PM Portal, click Create Order tab again. The order type is switched to "Return" with a purple border. A "Return Reason" textarea appears in the form.]

One last thing -- handling urgent same-day orders. If a crew is on site and realizes they are short on materials, open Create Order, set the priority to Urgent, set today's date, write "CREW WAITING" in special instructions, add the materials, and submit. Then immediately message the driver and the office in GroupMe. That is the fastest way to get materials to a site.

[SCREEN: The PM Portal dashboard, scrolled to the top.]

That is the PM Portal. Create orders with the right materials and delivery details, track your tickets, check inventory before ordering, and coordinate through GroupMe. You are the link between the office and the field -- this tool keeps everything connected.
