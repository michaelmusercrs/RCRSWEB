[SCREEN: Browser opening rivercityroofingsolutions.com/command-center/marketing. The Marketing Hub loads with a header reading "Marketing Hub - Q1 2026." Welcome to the RCRS Marketing Hub. This is your central command for everything marketing -- campaigns, content, ads, emails, and lead generation. When you log in, the Overview tab gives you the full picture. The four stat cards at the top show how many campaigns are active, how many email templates are ready, the total count of ad variations, and upcoming scheduled posts. Below that, your Q1 2026 Goals section tracks six key metrics with progress bars. When a goal hits 100 percent, you get a green checkmark. Scan these every morning to know which areas need your attention.

[SCREEN: Scroll to the Active Campaigns section. Four campaign cards display: "New Year Kickoff" (active, 75% progress), "3-Minute Financing" (active, 40% progress), "Storm Season Prep" (scheduled, 0% progress), and "Love Your Home" (scheduled, 0% progress). Each shows platforms, date range, status badge, and a progress bar. To the right, the Upcoming Content panel lists scheduled content items with type badges: Social in blue, Email in purple, Blog in amber, Ad in green.]

The Active Campaigns section shows every campaign in card form. You can see the campaign name, which platforms it runs on -- Facebook, Instagram, Google, Print -- the date range, and whether it is Active, Scheduled, or Completed. The Upcoming Content panel on the right previews what is coming next on the content calendar. Click "View all" to jump into the full calendar view.

[SCREEN: Click the "Ads" tab. The /command-center/marketing/ads page loads showing 10 ad variations organized by platform. Each ad shows the platform name, format (feed, story, carousel, reel, search, display, direct mail), preview of the creative, copy text, target audience, and performance metrics: impressions, clicks, CTR.]

Click the Ads tab to see all 10 ad variations across four platforms. Facebook has multiple formats -- feed posts, stories, and carousel. Instagram has stories, feed, and reels concepts. Google has search and display ads. And Print covers direct mail and flyers. Each ad shows its creative, copy, target audience, and performance data. Use this to A/B test variations -- run two versions and let the click-through rate tell you which one wins. Pause the underperformer and reallocate that budget to the winner.

[SCREEN: Click the "Emails" tab. The /command-center/marketing/emails page shows five email template cards: Welcome/Intro, Storm Season Alert, Inspection Follow-Up, Referral Program Promotion, and Seasonal Maintenance Tips. Each card displays the subject line, a preview snippet, and send stats (open rate, click rate).]

The Emails tab has five pre-built templates ready to go. Welcome and Intro for new leads. Storm Season Alert when severe weather is approaching. Inspection Follow-Up for homeowners who received a free roof inspection. Referral Program Promotion to drive word-of-mouth. And Seasonal Maintenance Tips for ongoing engagement. Each template shows its subject line, a content preview, and performance stats from past sends -- open rate and click rate. Click any template to customize the content, preview it, and schedule the send.

[SCREEN: Click the "Calendar" tab. The /command-center/marketing/calendar page shows a monthly calendar grid. Colored dots represent content types: blue for social posts, green for ad launches, purple for email sends, amber for blog posts. The current week is highlighted. Clicking a day reveals the scheduled items.]

The Content Calendar is your planning hub. Every piece of content is plotted on a monthly grid with color-coded dots. Blue is social media, green is ad launches, purple is email sends, and amber is blog posts. Click any day to see the full list of what is scheduled. Plan a month at a time so your campaigns, emails, blog posts, and social content all reinforce the same themes. When storm season arrives, your calendar should show storm blog posts, alert emails, and Check My Address promotions all running in the same week.

[SCREEN: Navigate to /portal/admin/blog. The Blog CMS loads showing a list of 68 articles. Each row shows title, date, status (Published or Draft), and category tag. A green "Write New Post" button is in the top right. An article title reads "What to Do After a Hail Storm Hits Your Roof."]

The Blog CMS is where you manage all 68 articles and create new ones. These articles drive organic search traffic across 367 pages -- they are one of our biggest SEO assets. Click "Write New Post" to create a new article. You get a title field, content editor, category selection, image upload from the Image Library, and SEO fields for title tag, meta description, and keywords. Every new article adds another indexed page to Google and another chance to rank for roofing-related searches in our markets. Prioritize topics that homeowners are searching for -- storm damage, insurance claims, roof maintenance, and local area content for Huntsville, Madison, Decatur, Birmingham, and Nashville.

[SCREEN: Navigate to /check-my-address. The public Check My Address page shows a clean form: street address, city, state, ZIP code, plus name, email, and phone fields. A large green "Get My Free Storm Report" button. The page is linked in the main website header and footer.]

This is your most powerful lead generation tool -- Check My Address. A homeowner enters their address and contact information, and the system queries real National Weather Service data to generate a storm risk report. They get a risk score from 0 to 100, a timeline of recent hail events with dates and sizes, risk factor explanations, and a recommendation. Meanwhile, their name, email, phone, and address are captured as a new lead in the system automatically. Promote this page everywhere: Facebook and Instagram ads, Google Ads, email campaigns, BNI presentations, door hangers, direct mail. The pitch is simple -- "Check your address for FREE and see if your roof may have storm damage." It provides real value to the homeowner and captures qualified leads for the team at the same time.

[SCREEN: Open a new browser tab showing analytics.google.com. The Google Analytics dashboard for the RCRS property (G-Y8PB85BZC5) loads showing a 30-day overview: total page views, sessions, users, average session duration. A traffic sources breakdown chart shows segments for Organic, Paid, Referral, Direct, and Social. A top pages table shows the most-viewed pages. A device breakdown shows mobile vs desktop percentages.]

Google Analytics is how you measure everything. Our tracking ID G-Y8PB85BZC5 is already installed on every page. Bookmark analytics.google.com and check it every morning. The overview shows total page views, sessions, unique users, and average time on site. The traffic sources breakdown tells you where visitors come from: organic search, paid ads, referrals, direct, and social media. Watch organic traffic grow as blog posts rank higher. Watch paid traffic spike when you launch new campaigns. Check which blog articles get the most views -- those topics are what homeowners are searching for, so write more like them. Monitor the mobile versus desktop split since most homeowners browse on phones. And track form submissions as conversions to see which channels are actually producing leads, not just clicks.

[SCREEN: Navigate to /portal/admin/areas. A list of 50+ service area cities with links. Huntsville, Madison, Decatur are at the top. Birmingham and Nashville are listed as expansion markets.]

One more tool for your SEO strategy -- Service Area pages. We have over 50 cities configured, each with a dedicated, locally optimized page that ranks for terms like "roofing company in Madison AL." As we expand into Birmingham and Nashville, adding a new service area page here is one of the fastest ways to start appearing in local search results. Combine a new area page with a targeted blog post and a Google Ads campaign, and you have a complete market-entry playbook.

[SCREEN: Back on the Marketing Hub overview page, scrolled to show the three Key Messaging Pillars at the bottom: Family-Owned Business, 3-Minute Financing, and Storm Damage Specialists.]

At the bottom of the Marketing Hub, the three Key Messaging Pillars are always visible: Family-Owned Business, 3-Minute Financing, and Storm Damage Specialists. Every piece of content you create should connect back to at least one of these three themes. They are what set RCRS apart.

That is your marketing toolkit. The Marketing Hub for campaigns, the Ad Library for creative management, the Email Center for nurture campaigns, the Content Calendar for planning, the Blog CMS for organic content, Check My Address for lead generation, Google Analytics for measurement, and Service Areas for local SEO. Check analytics every morning, plan content weekly, review campaign performance, and promote Check My Address constantly. You have 367 pages working for the business around the clock -- your job is to make sure the right people find them.
